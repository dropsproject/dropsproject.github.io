Chapter 6: Temporary Apostle (1)

Thump! Thump!

His heart was pounding.

‘I can start over. This time, for real, truly, properly...!’

At last, all his past efforts had not been in vain.

He now had the opportunity to start again.

Ihyeon turned around to thank Sun Wukong—

‘...Huh?’

Suddenly, the world around him began to spin.

「The Compliant Staff says you’re in the way, so take a nap, yeah? This trash body of yours will take a long time to fix into something human-like. How on earth did you even live this long with it?」

It seemed they were trying to put him into a comatose state for treatment and recovery, but there was a problem.

‘Wait a second, the dungeon’s about to collapse, so what’s going to happen to me...?’

A dreadful thought crossed Ihyeon’s mind, but his consciousness was already fading.

At that moment, the dungeon shook violently as if it were about to collapse.

Kwakwakwakwa...!

At the same time, countless messages began to appear without pause.


[You have successfully cleared all of the dungeon's quests. As a reward, you have obtained the ‘Spirit of ■■■■’.]

[You occupied 89% of the raid contribution. Having achieved an overwhelming first place, you have received an additional reward, the ‘Compliant Staff of ■■■■’.]

[You have gained a large amount of the Karma of ■■■■.]

[You have gained a large amount of the Karma of ■■■■.]

……

[Based on the Karma of ■■■■, the recovery speed of the Compliant Staff has greatly increased.]

……

[The curse ‘Ring of Bitter Cold’, left behind by the <Snow Queen>, has been completely lifted.]

[Your mana circuit has been restored, but the aftereffects remain severe.]

[Traces of the curse still linger throughout your mana circuit and body. Divine power is melting them away.]

……

[You have acquired Cold Resistance (S).]

[You have acquired Curse Resistance (S).]

……


[The dungeon is closing. All participants are requested to retreat to a safe zone.]

[10]

[9]

……

[0]


[Countdown has ended.]

[This dungeon is now closed.]


***


Beep, beep, beeeep—

A steady mechanical sound rang in his ears.

It was a sound he often heard whenever he was hospitalized.

Ihyeon barely opened his eyes.

‘Where is this...?’

He saw a white ceiling and white blankets.

A place all too familiar to him, like home. A hospital.

However, it was much larger than the usual room he stayed in. It seemed to be a private room.

Just then, the door opened.

“What, you’re awake? If you’re up, you should’ve pressed the call button right away. Why are you just lying there?”

A doctor in a white coat looked at Ihyeon with a gruff expression.

“Hyung.”

“What is it?”

Go Jin-u was one of the few people Ihyeon kept in touch with after his discharge from the military, and someone who was also close to Mun Suchan.

A man Ihyeon owed a lot to, he had originally graduated from the military academy and was one of the few medical officers in the Blue Thunder Unit.

“How did I end up here? I was definitely...”

“In a dungeon, right?”

Ihyeon quietly nodded.

“You remember sending the emergency signal, don’t you?”

“Yeah.”

No matter how well-prepared he was, Ihyeon knew that as long as his body remained weak, danger could come at any time inside a dungeon.

That was why, whenever he entered one, he always made sure to carry an emergency call item.

So that if anything happened to him, Go Jin-u would be alerted right away.


[Branch of the Intertwined Trees (Yeonriji)]

A twin accessory-type communicator crafted by splitting a branch from a ‘Yeonriji,’ a pair of trees said to grow intertwined as one.

When one side is touched or whispered to, the leaves on the other side tremble or vibrate in response. It only functions when there is trust or a bond between the two branches.

Origin: Folktale ‘The Yeonriji Tree’


The [Branch of the Intertwined Trees] was quite expensive, but its effect was undeniable.

Even though it had activated in the middle of the dungeon collapsing, it worked well enough for Go Jin-u to notice right away.

“The leaves were shaking so much that I knew something was up, so I immediately called the police. But before they arrived, White Burn showed up and brought you here?”

Ihyeon’s eyes widened slightly.

“White Burn?”

“Yeah, man. Do you know how shocked I was when Im Walpae herself came? What’s your relationship with her?”

[TL/N: Walpae, an old-fashioned Korean slang term referring to a rowdy troublemaker, ruffian, or bossy, strong-willed person, often with a rough personality but strong presence.]

“Im Walpae? Who’s that?”

“Oh, you don’t know that nickname? I’m talking about Im Wonyoung, yeah, that Im Wonyoung. She was a legend among my classmates. Said to knock out every guy who tried to confess to her.”

Ihyeon could easily imagine how everyone must have reacted when a beauty like Im Wonyoung entered the overwhelmingly male-dominated military academy.

‘Knowing Team Leader Im’s personality, she probably wouldn’t have stayed quiet about it.’

“Could you not look at me with those rotten, dead-fish eyes? I never confessed to her.”

“I didn’t say anything?”

“Then how about you wipe that impure look off your face before you speak?”

Ihyeon silently smirked.

Go Jin-u stared at him in disbelief.

“You damn near died, and now that I dragged you back from the gates of the underworld, this is how you act? Good grief.”

Clicking his tongue, Go Jin-u went on.

“Anyway, that Im Walpae kept insisting I had to save you no matter what. She nagged so much that even the section chief and the director came down in person. All the intern rookies nearly fainted. It was chaos.”

Only then did Ihyeon understand how things had gone.

‘So she personally led the rescue team to find me.’

It seemed that Im Wonyoung had returned with the White Burn reinforcements and found Ihyeon abandoned outside the dungeon gate.

“But seriously, what’s your relationship with Im Walpae?”

“Nothing at all.”

“You expect me to believe that?”

“Then don’t.”

“Unbelievable. The way you talk.”

“That’s because it’s the truth. Today was only the second time we’ve met.”

“And yet she took such good care of you?”

Ihyeon shrugged.

“I’m just that charming.”

“This is insane. You’re so full of yourself, really.”

“When I was in the unit, if they’d held a popularity vote among the female members, don’t you think I’d have won easily?”

“Yeah, that’s only because you were the leader, you idiot! They were just blinded by…”

“It’s kind of pathetic to say that when you know you wouldn’t get a single vote.”

“Fuck you!”

“And by the way, I wasn’t even the leader then. I was a rookie.”

“Shut up!”

Go Jin-u yelled, realizing he’d only managed to work himself up by arguing pointlessly with Ihyeon.

“Anyway, after you went into recovery, Im Walpae went back too. She said you should return the thing she lent you. What’s that about? You owe her something?”

“There’s something like that.”

Ihyeon gave a small laugh and rubbed his left index finger with his right hand. The [Amulet of Protection] radiated a gentle warmth that seemed to caress him.

At that moment—

Fwoooosh!

‘My mana... it’s moving.’

Ihyeon’s eyes gleamed as he felt his mana flow toward the [Amulet of Protection].

Even though the amount was still far too small to truly be called mana, and his frozen mana circuits remained stiff and cold—

The sensation of mana flowing through his whole body was indescribably comforting.

“And when you get something good later, share it with me too, you punk. I was told this room’s yours until you’re fully recovered, so get some rest. I’m off.”

Go Jin-u seemed to have noticed the change in Ihyeon. Though he still sounded indifferent, his voice carried a note of relief as he left the room.

Once Ihyeon was alone, he quickly began checking every nook and cranny of his body.

And then—


[Using ‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ to summon the user’s status window.]


[Jang Ihyeon]

—Age: 27 years old.

—Traits: Leader, Indomitable, Frostbitten Human, [Locked], [Locked]

—Stats

Strength: 17

Agility: 15

Stamina: 14

Mana: 4

Intelligence: 19

Dexterity: 18

—Divine Authority: Fiery Golden Eyes, [Locked]

—Skills: [Locked], [Locked], [Locked], [Locked], [Locked]

—Resistance: Cold (S), Curse (S)

—Current Status: All curses have been lifted. However, due to long-term aftereffects, overall stats have significantly decreased.

—Remarks: Some mana coefficients have been partially restored, allowing limited use of magic.


Ihyeon clenched his fist tightly as he looked at the ‘’Mana: 4’ displayed on his status window.

Although both his Cold Resistance and Curse Resistance had reached S rank, it was this seemingly insignificant change that resonated with him more deeply.

‘So this is... the true power of a sacred relic, and not just any relic, but one of mythic grade.’

Even though the Compliant Staff was said to have most of its functions sealed, it was already this powerful.

Just how incredible would it be once all its abilities were fully released?

It was then.

「Well, well? That’s quite a moved expression you’ve got there. If I help you a second time, would this place turn into a river of tears?」

He turned his head in his direction.

The white monkey, Sun Wukong, was sitting on the bed railing with a sly grin, his tail swaying lightly.

“Great Sage!”

「Do you like your body now? That guy went through a lot of trouble working on it all night.」

Ziiiiing!

The Compliant Staff wrapped around his right wrist vibrated again.

Only after feeling that vibration—

Did Ihyeon finally realize that this long-awaited moment was, indeed, reality.


***


How many hours had passed since then?

Ihyeon slowly opened his eyes.

Flash!

A golden light flickered briefly above his eyes, then disappeared.

At that moment, a message appeared.


[You have successfully unlocked the skill ‘Dueokshini’s Breath (F)’, which had been sealed until now, by partially restoring your mana.]


[Dueokshini’s Breath (F)]

Like a vengeful dueokshini bursting with suppressed emotions in the middle of the night, the magic infused with fervent desire and vivid imagery is unleashed in the form of lightning, moving as if alive like a Dueokshini.

—Type: Skill, Mana Control

—Proficiency: 0%

—Origin: Traditional tale ‘Curse of the Chilseong Rock’


[Used ‘Skill: Dueokshini’s Breath (F)’ to gather the divine power scattered throughout the mana circuit.]

[A large amount of divine power has been stored in the core. Proficiency has greatly increased.]

[Dueokshini’s Breath (F) has advanced to Grade E.]

[Dueokshini’s Breath (E) has advanced to Grade D.]

……

[Stored mana begins to flow, accelerating the removal of the curse's aftereffects.]


Ihyeon gathered his mana to the fullest and spread his palm wide open.

Crackle! Crack!

Then, at the same time, blue-white sparks leapt up from his palm.

Although they were small, like static electricity, they moved so freely as if possessed by a ghost.

「Is that the ability you originally had?」

“Yes. It’s been a while since I last used it, so it’s not easy to control.”

「You had quite a decent mana control skill, huh?」

Sun Wukong watched Ihyeon with curious eyes as he recovered much faster than he expected and even began releasing his powers.

Even if Ihyeon still retained divine power and karma, this was far quicker than he anticipated.

Ihyeon nodded.

“It’s actually the skill that essentially made me who I was before.”

The [Dueokshini’s Breath] was a skill Ihyeon had inherited from his mentor during his time in the military.

Depending on the intensity of his emotions, it could make his mana explode with immense destructive power or be controlled delicately for precise work.

It was so exceptional that it had been classified as a top-tier confidential skill within the unit. When Ihyeon was first granted permission to use it, the decision had caused considerable opposition within the ranks.

But with his mentor’s full support, Ihyeon became the leader of the Blue Thunder Unit and silenced all opposition.

At the time, the blue-white thunderstorm he commanded was so overwhelming that it became the very symbol of the Blue Thunder Unit.

“But I still have a long way to go. I wonder when its authority will awaken.”

What Ihyeon needed most was the authority that would be unlocked when Dueokshini’s Breath reached completion.

It was his signature move, the very technique that had made Jet who he was and had once allowed him to defeat the Snow Queen.

‘Only when I can activate Thunder Demon (Noegwi) will I finally be able to fight on equal terms.’

During the past four years, his enemies had already climbed far above him.

‘Still, I have many options. Especially with the ice crystals remaining throughout my mana circuits.’

The aftereffects left behind by the Ring of Bitter Cold. Though the curse attribute that once tormented him had vanished, clumps of mana remained frozen within his circuits like hardened ice.

Ihyeon had named them ice crystals.

‘If I can melt all of these and fully merge them into my mana circuits, my mana capacity will increase significantly.’

Ultimately, in order to achieve even faster and greater growth like this…….

“First, what should I do to be truly acknowledged by the Compliant Staff?”

「You’re planning to move already? Why not rest a bit longer?」

“I’ve had more than enough rest these past four years.”

Sun Wukong found Ihyeon’s determination quite pleasing.

「Sorry to say this, but no matter how much you struggle right now, you can’t become the chosen wielder of the Compliant Staff. Do you think its past masters were the kind of men you could take lightly?」

Ihyeon recalled the names written in the staff’s description.

Pangu, Yu, Taishang Laojun, Sun Wukong...

Each one a being of divine rank, names that held absolute weight in Eastern mythology.

Thinking about it that way, he felt infinitely miserable as a mere mortal.

「Even the Dragon King of the East Sea couldn’t be chosen, and the staff just rotted away in his vault.」

“Then what should I do?”

「You must grow stronger, steadily but in a different way from before. At the very least, you need to lose that ‘temporary’ title.」

At that moment, something flashed through Ihyeon’s mind.

“Before the Compliant Staff, I should first make the Fiery Golden Eyes completely my own.”

「Now, aren’t we finally on the same page?」

For the first time, Sun Wukong smiled with satisfaction.

Until now, the Fiery Golden Eyes had neither fully accepted nor rejected Ihyeon.

Due to the will left behind by its original master, Mun Suchan, the sacred relic had continued to assist Ihyeon according to his will.

But now that Ihyeon had regained his mana and was preparing to travel the world in search of Sun Wukong’s scattered myths, things had to change.

It was time for the relationship to become one of true ownership between the wielder and the sacred relic, not one bound by a lingering will.

‘To achieve that, I’ll need to absorb as much of Sun Wukong’s karma as possible and raise my rank. Only then will I be able to fully own the Fiery Golden Eyes.’

Only with [eyes] that evolved in an extraordinary way could he track down the scattered myths of Sun Wukong.

It was a cycle, each feeding into the other.

Being chosen by the Compliant Staff could come later.

‘The Compliant Staff isn’t the only one. There are other sacred relics that symbolize Sun Wukong. The Cloud Somersault, the Golden Circlet, the Transformation Art... I need to obtain all of them.’

Maybe because his thoughts reached that far?

Suddenly, Ihyeon’s mind wandered to what came next.

‘If I were to claim all of Sun Wukong’s karma and sacred relics, and reclaim Jet’s authority, what would happen then?’

No, not just reclaiming them. I shouldn’t stop there. What if I rebuilt Jet’s authority from the ground up, improving what had once fallen short?

Then—

‘Could I perhaps ascend to a higher existence?’

Like the top rankers he had seen in South Africa.

Or maybe—

‘Like Sun Wukong himself…!’

Ihyeon clenched his fist tightly.

There were many paths ahead of him now.

But he had no intention of rushing or making careless mistakes.

‘Step by step, from the beginning. Steadily.’

That was exactly when it happened.

Fwoooosh!

“...!?”

Suddenly, mana stirred within his core, surged upward, and poured into his eyes.

For the first time, the Fiery Golden Eyes accepted Ihyeon’s mana.


[‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ has absorbed mana and unlocked a new function.]

[Searching for the scattered myths of ■■■■.]

……

[Search in progress.]

……

[Search complete.]

[One has been detected nearby.]

[Proceed to Cheongnyangni Station.]


Ihyeon’s eyes widened.
