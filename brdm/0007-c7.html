Chapter 7: Temporary Apostle (2)

Ihyeon requested permission to be discharged from the hospital.

The hospital staff tried to stop him, saying his tests were not yet finished, but Ihyeon was too stubborn. In the end, he was allowed to go out only because Go Jin-u agreed to take responsibility for him.

「You’ve got a good friend, haven’t you?」

“He’s one of the few people left who had a connection with my late brother Suchan. He was closest to him.”

「Hmm.」

Sun Wukong looked as if he were mulling over the name of someone who had almost become his apostle.

It was impossible to tell what he was truly thinking.

“But, is Great Sage visible to anyone else?”

In truth, what Ihyeon worried about most when bringing Sun Wukong out with him was Sun Wukong himself.

He had gotten used to it by now, but if a white monkey went around talking in front of others, it would cause quite a scene.

Fortunately, Sun Wukong was completely invisible to anyone except Ihyeon.

「Because of the lack of connection (yeon).」

“You mean they can’t see you because they have no link to you?”

「That’s right. Normally, you wouldn’t be able to see me either. But you’ve taken a great deal of my karma, and you have the [eyes], so you can. You even presided over the ritual that summoned me.」

Later, he learned that serving as a priest at the royal tomb had greatly helped him be appointed as a temporary apostle.

Ihyeon had forged a connection with Sun Wukong that had not originally existed.

「So, where exactly are we going? This isn’t the direction of my myth, is it?」

“I’m thinking of stopping by Jongno 3rd street first.”

「What’s there?」

“My alter ego.”

「……?」

Sun Wukong tilted his head.

“We’re here.”

Then, seeing where Ihyeon had stopped, his eyes gleamed.

「A forge?」

“A hardware store.”

Sun Wukong was a martial deity who pursued his own form of martial strength.

Naturally, he was very interested in all kinds of weapons.

Moreover, in this era when dungeons filled with myths and legends had begun to run rampant again, modern reinterpretations had produced an endless variety of arms and weapons.

「So, this alter ego of yours, is it something you once used…?」

“Yes. It’s my weapon. Since I’ve regained my mana, I thought I might be able to use it again.”

「Oh?」

“I did obtain the Compliant Staff, but right now I can’t actually use it as a weapon.”

「Heh! Fair enough. You’re not a full-fledged apostle yet, after all. I guess until then, you’ll really need something to fight with, won’t you?」

At present, the Compliant Staff could only perform [Rapid Recovery] and [Metabolic Regulation].

Of course, those alone were remarkable functions, but compared to the true power of the Compliant Staff, they were merely fragments.

Which was why he needed a real weapon.

The one used by Jet.

‘A weapon, huh… I wonder what kind it’ll be.’

From what Sun Wukong had observed, Ihyeon was a hunter with extraordinary instincts.

He also seemed quite skilled at handling weapons, so Sun Wukong was very curious about what kind he had used.

‘I just hope it matches well with that strange lightning mana of his, that Dueokshini or whatever it was.’

Sun Wukong chuckled, recalling Ihyeon’s mana, which carried the nature of thunder yet felt shadowy and sharp, like something lurking in the dark.

“Excuse me.”

Ihyeon entered the shabbiest of the hardware shops there.

It didn’t even have a proper signboard.

However—

Whoosh!

The moment he opened the thick iron door, a wave of hot air swept over him.

Inside, a tanned old man was hammering metal.

Clang! Clang! Clang!

Even with a customer present, he remained completely focused on his work.

“Sir, I’m here to pick up something I left with you before.”

Clang! Clang!

“I’ll find it myself, all right?”

Clang! Clang!

“Thank you for your hard work.”

Claang! Claang!

Without waiting for an answer, Ihyeon walked toward a storage area in the corner.

「…What is with that man?」

Only after the wave of heat subsided did Sun Wukong exhale the breath he’d been holding.

「Why does he reek so strongly of Dochul’s energy?」


[Four Fiends (Sahung) Taotie (Dochul)]

A monster that devours everything in its path, consuming whatever it finds. Among all things, it has a particular preference for metal.

The metal it excretes is a compound of all that it has eaten, producing a wide variety of types and materials.

—Origin: Legend ‘Classic of Mountains and Seas, Bestiary of Monsters’


Through his own Fiery Golden Eyes, Sun Wukong could glimpse countless legends and myths surrounding the old man who had been hammering metal.

It was far beyond the skill of an ordinary craftsman.

“He was the one who made weapons for the unit I used to belong to. It should be around here somewhere… Found it.”

Ihyeon managed to find a box wedged in one corner among the haphazard piles of boxes and scrap metal.

It was so covered in dust that even the name written on its surface was hard to make out, but to Ihyeon, it was unmistakably familiar.

Because four years ago, he had entrusted it to the old man with tears in his eyes, promising that he would come back for it no matter what.

「A producer, you say? That doesn’t make any sense…!」

“Of course, he didn’t make weapons for just anyone. I don’t know what his standards were, but he only crafted for members who truly earned his approval.”

With eyes gleaming, Ihyeon tore off the tape sealing the box.

“And this is the first custom-made item the old man crafted for me back then.”

The moment he threw open the box, a neatly folded Blue Thunder Unit uniform and three hand axes resting on top of it came into view.

They were modern-style axes often used even for camping.


[Dochul’s Fangs]

Axes imbued with the gluttony and greed that the fiend beast Dochul takes pride in. Each axe constantly craves metal, flesh, and blood.

—Type: Axe. One-handed weapon.

—Origin: Traditional tale ‘Four Fiends, Dochul’


「Very fierce ones, aren’t they.」

Sun Wukong checked the history of the three axes and widened his eyes.

“Yes. From the very beginning, they were created to maximize the properties of [Dueokshini’s Breath]. That’s why.”

Each of [Dochul’s Fangs] had a name: Sikcheol (Iron-Eater), Galyuk (Flesh-Thirst), and Gyohyeol (Blood-Biter).

They meant to eat iron, crave flesh, and tear blood.

As cruel as their names suggest, they were crafted solely for killing.

That was why they sometimes even threatened their own master, Ihyeon himself.

However, they were perfectly compatible with [Dueokshini’s Breath].

The more his suppressed emotions burst forth, the stronger the skill became. When that energy was channeled through his hand axes, the resulting destruction was beyond imagination.

Blue-white lightning rained down as if to shatter the world.

It was those very axes that had once slaughtered the Snow Queen.

‘Though they were badly damaged back then, and the old man practically had to rebuild them from scratch, their power remains the same.’

Ihyeon gently ran his hand over the axes for the first time in a long while.

‘No, they’ve actually become even stronger.’

Even though he only brushed the spine where there was no edge, a small cut appeared on his index finger, and blood trickled down.

As Ihyeon put his finger into his mouth, his eyes gleamed.

‘It must’ve drawn out the grudges I held against the Snow Queen, still lingering on the axes.’

Spit.

Letting out a faint chuckle without realizing it, Ihyeon placed the axes into his bag along with his storage gear.

“Fortunately, it looks like I can still use them, though barely.”

「You said you could only handle them back when you were a Ranker. What if it’s not as easy now?」

“Isn’t that even better?”

「Hm?」

“The fiercer they are, the quicker my skills will return. I’ll have to stay sharp at all times so I don’t get devoured. That’ll be good training too.”

「Ha, seriously.」

Sun Wukong gave him a look as if he were watching a madman but said nothing more.

After all, since ancient times, for a martial artist, a bit of madness was what made one stronger. He had been like that once himself.

“That’s why I didn’t bring any other protective gear. I figured I should train my body first.”

Ihyeon touched several of the custom items that had once symbolized Jet, then closed the box lid and pushed it back into place.

‘Someday.’

Inside were many items far more vicious and of higher grade than [Dochul’s Fangs]. There were even sacred relics.

But until he finished his preparations, Ihyeon had no intention of returning here again.

“Sir, I’m heading out.”

He gave the old man a light bow and left the forge.

Even then, the old man said nothing and simply kept hammering away.

Taaang! Taaang!!


***


『Next stop, Cheongnyangni. Cheongnyangni Station. The doors will open on…….』

Ihyeon exited the subway station and looked around.

Perhaps because it was a transfer station, a large crowd was constantly moving in all directions.

“I came because it told me to, but… is there really a dungeon in a place like this? No matter how I look at it, this doesn’t seem like somewhere one could exist.”

When a dungeon appeared, the entire area within a one-kilometer radius was always subject to a full clearance operation by regulation.

There were no exceptions, even in busy districts. This was to prevent casualties in case of a Break outbreak.

So hearing that one existed here was naturally confusing.

「Let’s go take a look first, shall we? More importantly, brat.」

“……?”

「Before we head to the testing site, let’s stop by there first.」

“……??”

The place Sun Wukong pointed to was a fish-shaped pastry stall.

Ihyeon wondered why he was acting like this all of a sudden, but Sun Wukong’s gaze toward the stall was unusually serious.

「Let’s see. There are three kinds. Red bean, custard, and sweet potato cheese? Oh, that’s an interesting combination. What are you doing? Go on, buy them already. Get every flavor! Three of each.」

“You’re going to eat them?”

「What else would I do?」

Sun Wukong looked at Ihyeon as if he had just asked the dumbest question in the world.

Ihyeon was speechless but still bought the pastries and handed them to Sun Wukong.

「I saw these before, when you were being hauled off to the hospital by that bunch from White Burn or whatever they were called. The smell was amazing, so I’ve been curious ever since. The taste is… ohhh.」

Ihyeon stared blankly at Sun Wukong, whose eyes sparkled with pure delight as if to say, ‘How can something in this world taste this incredible!’

‘Wait, isn’t he supposed to be a spirit? How does that even go inside? And how does digestion work?’

Shaking his head, Ihyeon simply followed the direction shown by the Fiery Golden Eyes.


[Move west.]

[Move northeast.]

……


The place he was guided to, as if he had some kind of navigator, was Gyeongdong Market, not far from Cheongnyangni Station.

Among them, there was a Herbal Medicine Market located on the east side.

The scent of various medicinal herbs, including ginseng, filled the air.

And then—

‘A door?’

In the distance, between two stalls, he spotted an old, shabby aluminum door.

From the rectangular gap in the door, a golden light, the same color as the Compliant Staff, was leaking out.

‘Is it here?’

Ihyeon stood before the door and looked around.

Fortunately, it seemed that no one else could see the light.

He lightly placed his hand on the doorknob.

Paaaat!

At that moment, the Compliant Staff burst into a radiant halo, and the golden glow seeping through the gap vanished.

Then—

Clack!

Creak—

With the sound of the lock opening, the door slowly swung open.

Before anyone could notice, Ihyeon stepped inside. A halo of light enveloped him.

Hwaaak!

The same sensation as when he passed through a gate.

When he opened his eyes again, Ihyeon was no longer in Gyeongdong Market, but in a new space.

‘A dungeon!’


[You have entered an ‘Instance Dungeon: Yellow Desert.’]

[You have entered an incomplete fragment of ■■■■.]

[This is a desert where only the wind of death blows, stealing the breath and warmth of life in every season.]

[Overcome all obstacles and cross the desert to reach the oasis. The sweet water of the oasis will awaken your mind with clarity.]

[Upon success: ■■■■’s karma.]

[Upon failure: Death by heatstroke (Gal-sa).]


A yellow desert of dark, dusky hue stretched endlessly along the horizon.

The scorching heat and dry wind awakened Ihyeon’s senses once more.

“Is this a personalized dungeon made just for me or something like that?”

Since he had never heard of the concept of an instance dungeon before, that was the only thought that came to mind.

「Mumble, mumble, a myth, mumble, mumble, like that, mumble, mumble.」

“Please finish eating before you talk.”

Sun Wukong said that while tossing the tail of the fish-shaped pastry into his mouth.

「Strictly speaking, this part comes first. Based on the <Fragment> of the myth I possessed, a <Story> is created. Once the structure of the story is completed, a [Gate] that connects to the outside world is formed.」

Sun Wukong continued with a sly grin.

「In other words, before a [Gate] was even made, you created a [Temporary Gate] and came through. Through the [Eyes]. You could say a special passage suited to you was formed.」

Ihyeon realized that the [Gate] Sun Wukong mentioned referred to the entrance or passage that leads him into this dungeon.

“Then I’ll need to clear it before the story structure is completed. It wouldn’t be good if the dungeon appeared in the outside world.”

「Exactly. So you’d better hurry, right?」

Ihyeon silently nodded. He felt as though he had glimpsed a secret about dungeons that had never been revealed to the world.

At the same time, he thought that from now on, when recovering Sun Wukong’s myths, he wouldn’t have to worry about interference from others.

As long as it wasn’t a completed dungeon with a formed gate like <Monkey Den>, he could simply clear it beforehand.

Of course, thinking in reverse, this also meant he wouldn’t know exactly what kind of <Story> the dungeon contained.

And there was nothing more dangerous than a dungeon whose <Story> was unknown.

However—

‘That’s exactly what a pathfinder’s role is meant to handle.’

Ihyeon opened his Fiery Golden Eyes wide.

The mana of Dueokshini’s Breath, which he had trained overnight, seeped into his eyes and turned them golden.


[You’ve begun the dungeon investigation through the mana-enhanced ‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes.’]

[All senses are fully open.]

[Beyond sight, synesthesia begins to perceive and interpret the information of the surrounding world.]


What Ihyeon learned for the first time was that the Fiery Golden Eyes, when filled with mana, were not limited to sight alone.

From sight to hearing, to smell, to touch, to taste, all five senses merged into one synesthetic perception.

And eventually, it could even open up extrasensory perception (chogamgak), or a sixth sense (yukgam), beyond the five senses.

Since Ihyeon already had exceptionally sharp [Sense] honed on the battlefield—

Connecting [Sense] with [Eyes] and then integrating them together was a remarkable achievement.

Thanks to that—

He could clearly perceive even distant areas beyond his line of sight.

Pah!

Through the sandstorm that suddenly swept in and obscured his vision, he quickly detected sharp, glinting fangs.

His reaction was instantaneous.

One of the Dochul’s Fangs hanging from his waist flew forward.

The throwing speed was so fast it was nearly impossible to follow with the naked eye.

Puhk!

Yelp! Yipe!

Something burst through the sandstorm, bounced roughly off the ground, and rolled to a stop.

A wolf the size of a person lay dead, a hand axe buried between its brows.

‘Wolves move in packs.’

Ihyeon reached for his waist again and scanned his surroundings, where his vision remained obscured.

The battle had already begun.

「Yellow wolves, they’re annoying to deal with. They often hide in sandstorms, so they’re not easy to find.」

Sun Wukong was smiling brightly, watching the fight from above Ihyeon’s head as if enjoying the show.

Although his smug expression was slightly irritating, Ihyeon ignored it and spoke while keeping his eyes on the surroundings.

“Yes, they are troublesome. But more importantly, Great Sage.”

「What is it?」

“With all this sand blowing around, is your fish-shaped pastry all right?”

「...!?」

The fish-shaped pastry he had been saving was already covered in sand.
