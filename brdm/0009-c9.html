Chapter 9: Temporary Apostle (4)

[You have crossed half of the desert.]

[Progress Rate: 47%]


Bang!

Thud!

Boasting its massive size, the desert snake tried to coil tightly around Ihyeon, but he struck its head down in a single, swift motion.

He had nearly lost consciousness for a moment when its fangs pierced him, a dizzying experience that almost made his mind go blank, but—


[Poison Resistance (F) has been acquired. You can now withstand poison more effectively.]

[Stamina has increased by 1.]


Thanks to his increased endurance from higher stamina, he somehow managed to hold out and sever the creature’s neck with his kukri.

Thud!

A head the size of a human body fell to the ground, its tongue lolling out.

A torrent of blood gushed forth, soaking the dry desert.

“Haa... haa... I really almost died this time.”

Barely able to catch his breath, Ihyeon collapsed weakly onto the ground.


[Excessive fatigue has accumulated. Anemia and dizziness are setting in. Rest is advised.]

[Fatigue Level: 73]

[Severe blood loss detected. Supply nutrients and rest. Bleeding Resistance (E) has advanced to grade D.]

[You have withstood the Desert Snake’s ‘Constrict.’ Physical Resistance (F) has advanced to grade E.]


「You crazy bastard, are you really planning to die?」

Sun Wukong descended, looking exhausted.

Throughout the entire dungeon raid, which was nearly halfway done, Ihyeon had been marching without rest.


[You have cleared a total of 6 monster dens so far.]

[The monsters of the desert have begun to fear you. You have earned the title ‘Desert Slayer’.]

[You can now better withstand the desert’s heat and scorching temperatures.]


Through the Fiery Golden Eyes, he confirmed that the water sources (suwon) of each lair were connected through underground streams, and while tracing them back to find a path—

He had continued fighting monsters almost without rest.

“Still, thanks to that, I’ve gained Poison Resistance, didn’t I? And two other resistances even advanced.”

「And if you die doing that, it’ll all be for nothing, won’t it?」

“I have divine power, don’t I? Plus, the Compliant Staff has healing abilities.”

「…You’re seriously out of your mind.」

Ihyeon did not regret his choice.

Just looking at his status window was enough to make him feel content.


[Using ‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ to summon the user’s status window.]


[Jang Ihyeon]

—Age: 27

—Traits: Leader, Indomitable, Frostbitten Human, [Locked], [Locked]

—Stats

Strength: 42

Agility: 31

Stamina: 30

Mana: 15

Intelligence: 37

Dexterity: 30

—Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes, [Locked]

—Skills: Dueokshini’s Breath (C), Combat Instinct (E), [Locked], [Locked], [Locked]

—Resistances: Cold (S), Curse (S), Pain (D), Bleeding (D), Paralysis (E), Physical (E), Fatigue (D), Poison (F)

—Current Status: You have gained too many resistances in a short period of time, causing your overall metabolism to fall into an unbalanced state.

—Remarks: Rapidly recovering lost stats. Compared to your previous averages, sensory sharpness has increased significantly.


In just a few hours, his stats had nearly doubled.

On top of that, his status window was now filled with resistances.

The speed of his growth was clearly remarkable. He almost wanted to push himself even harder.

“Look. My leg doesn’t hurt anymore.”

Ihyeon smiled brightly as he showed that his right leg now moved exactly as he wanted.

Of course, to Sun Wukong, that grin was nothing but irritating.

「No matter how much the Compliant Staff enhances your recovery speed, it only amplifies your natural healing ability.」

“Then maybe I'll go gain some recovery-related skills.”

「Do you think gaining a skill is that easy?」

“Then what else can I do? I’ll just have to push myself harder. Oh, and maybe I’ll develop a resistance related to it as well? That actually sounds great, right?”

「Cough! That’s not what I meant!」

Sun Wukong was exasperated by how Ihyeon always turned every bit of advice into another chance for training.

‘Wait, didn’t he say he couldn’t even run properly for four years because of the curse? How in the world did he endure that all this time!?’

With a personality so obsessed with self-discipline, those years must have been hell.

Even if this was some kind of rebound from the curse, it was still too extreme.

‘Or maybe… the opposite?’

Then, seeing the calm and composed look in Ihyeon’s eyes, Sun Wukong finally realized his mistake.

‘So he had been training on his own all along! That’s why he regained his senses so quickly!’

Ihyeon had never gone a single day without training.

Even while suffering from the curse, even through despair and hopelessness, he had always strengthened his body as much as his condition allowed.

That was why he could drag his weakened body through the dungeon and awaken Sun Wukong.

‘He’s had faith in himself.’

Faith that he would stand up again someday.

Conviction that he had to be ready when the opportunity came.

Sun Wukong had no choice but to acknowledge him, even if just a little.

‘Even though he’s the one pushing harder, I’m the one worrying more. Hmph.’

“It seems you’re not as doubtful of me as before.”

「Quiet, you brat. The only reason I’m holding back is because recruiting a new apostle takes too much time! I just can’t stand tedious things!」

Of course, he had no intention of admitting that honestly.

But Ihyeon grinned as if he already knew what he was thinking, which made Sun Wukong want to smack him on the head just once.

“Then how about you teach me a skill instead?”

「What?」

“As a Great Sage, you must have plenty of techniques, right? If there’s one related to physical combat, please teach me. That way you won’t have to worry so much, don’t you think that would be great?”

Sun Wukong frowned.

「So you’re trying to extort me, huh?」

“I’ve proven that I’m useful enough as a temporary apostle. I’m just asking for a small reward.”

「Ha! Seriously, that mouth of yours...!」

Ihyeon quickly changed the subject.

“The fish-shaped pastries were good, weren’t they? Once we’re out, I’ll buy you something even tastier.”

At that moment, Sun Wukong’s ears twitched.

「...Something tastier than fish-shaped pastries?」

‘Knew it.’

Ihyeon smiled inwardly.

He had noticed how Sun Wukong remained attached to that sand-covered fish pastry, so he tried probing just in case.

Apparently, his tastes were just as childish as his little monkey appearance.

“Do you know what a fat macaron is?”

「Fat macaron?」

“How about ice cream hotteok or rose tteokbokki?”

「I-I know what hotteok is, but it comes with ice cream on top? No, more importantly, what kind of combination is that, rose tteokbokki…!」

“There’s a saying, dan-jjan dan-jjan (sweet and salty, sweet and salty). It means that after eating something sweet, you should have something salty, and then something sweet again, so that your tongue can say, ‘Ah, what a pleasant day.’”

「...!?」

“So let’s get out of here soon and go enjoy that, shall we?”

Sun Wukong just stood there blankly for a moment. Just hearing the explanation made his mouth water slightly.

Then, realizing Ihyeon was watching him with a teasing grin, he coughed and composed himself.

「...Ahem! Seeing your admirable determination to keep learning even while crossing the desert, I’ve decided to bestow upon you a small technique. Watch and learn well.」

“Yes, thank you.”

「Don’t misunderstand. This has nothing to do with food.」

“Of course. No one would think otherwise.”

Sun Wukong gave him a doubtful look, then—

Tap!

He straightened his posture and lightly stepped on the ground.

A metal staff-like object shot up from the earth and landed neatly in his hand. Even if food wasn’t involved, Sun Wukong now thought it might be fine to start passing down some of his teachings to Ihyeon.

「From what I’ve seen, you rely a lot on your senses and intuition in combat. You also seem to specialize in lightning-type techniques. In that case, how about learning a skill from that Zeus fellow instead of mine?」

A name Ihyeon had never expected to hear.


Jeuseu (Zeus).


The leader of <Olympus>, a group of gods so great their number could be counted on one hand.

Ihyeon’s eyes widened slightly.

“Zeus belongs to a completely different mythology from the Great Sage, right?”

「There was a time I made a bet and took away one of his divine powers.」

“……!”

Ihyeon clenched his fist without realizing it.

He had only tried to see if he could start learning some techniques from Sun Wukong—

But to think the pumpkin that rolled toward him turned out to be much bigger than expected.

「And it’s not just him. The Jade Emperor, Odin, Osiris, Brahma... all their main powers and skills are contained within this.」

Sun Wukong grinned playfully and tapped his small head with his index finger.

Ihyeon could almost imagine what kind of humiliation those gods, including Zeus, must have suffered at Sun Wukong’s hands.

Perhaps this was even connected to the conflict that had broken out between him and the other deities…

‘Zeus, as one of the main gods, is said to be among the few who still have no apostles.’

Thump! Thump!

His heart pounded.

「Of course, without a sacred relic like Astrafe or Zeus’s personal favor, it’s just a half-measure of a half-measure, with limited output...!」

Crackle! Crack!

In an instant, bright yellow lightning began to spark around Sun Wukong.

It resembled the Fiery Golden Eyes, but its hue was closer to sunlight.

「Still, for someone who’s only just learning to walk, this is quite the blessing. Don’t you think?」

Ihyeon straightened his posture.

“Yes. I’ll watch carefully and learn.”

「Teaching my apostle the technique of an enemy god... Heh, that sounds like a fun kind of revenge.」

Maybe that casual mutter revealed his true motive.

「Anyway, keep up and watch closely. Who knows? If I feel generous, I might throw in not just my skills but also the powers and skills of the others too. Want that?」

“……That sounds like you’re torturing me with false hope. Sure. But for now, I’ll focus on learning this properly.”

「Ha ha, good attitude. If you had gotten greedy without knowing your limits, I would have smashed your skull first.」

Cold sweat ran down Ihyeon’s back as he gave a dry laugh.

It seemed this was another one of Sun Wukong’s tests.

But that thought quickly faded.

「Keep both eyes open and watch carefully. I won’t show this to you twice.」

Sun Wukong lightly thrust his staff forward with all his strength.

A flawless motion.

But the result was anything but clean.

Kwoom!

Lightning gathered at the tip of the staff and exploded.

It was like a pillar of thunder piercing the sky.

Sand burst upward violently.

“……!”

Crackle! Crackle!

A wave of scorching heat struck Ihyeon’s face.

His skin tingled.

‘……The lightning feels alive.’

「The way you use your lightning energy focuses only on release. The more your emotions surge, the more you pour it out, right?」

“……Yes.”

「But that’s why it discharges (bangjeon) so quickly, isn’t it? That must have been your biggest problem.」

“Yes. Everything you said is true.”

Ihyeon nodded heavily.

In truth, his fights never lasted long.

It was because the emotional strain that fueled his lightning was far too severe.

After releasing all that emotion in battle, an overwhelming fatigue always followed, forcing him to endure grueling recovery periods.

‘That was the problem even when I fought the Snow Queen.’

Sun Wukong had seen that weakness at a glance.

「But Zeus, that stingy bastard, would never waste his energy like that. He’ll try to save it again and again no matter what. He always aims to draw out maximum efficiency with minimal output.」

Sun Wukong intended to pass down only the efficiency aspect of this technique to his temporary apostle.

「So the most important point in Zeus’s technique is condensation.」

“Condensation……”

「Yes. Instead of just spreading the lightning out, you compress it as much as possible, to the absolute limit, and then—」

Crackle! Crackle!

Once again, lightning flared from the tip of his staff.

It spread out like a spiderweb, dyeing the surroundings in bright yellow light—

Then suddenly it spiraled inward, shrinking until it became a pillar, and finally condensed into a single sphere.

Like a miniature sun, it radiated tremendous heat.

「The key is to make it explode right at the point of impact.」

Sun Wukong struck the sphere with his staff.

The lightning, compressed to its limit and burning intensely, burst in every direction, creating a massive explosion.

Kwoom! Kwooroom! Kwoooroom!

Kwoorooroom...!

Dozens of lightning strikes slammed into the sand, and sparks that scattered across the ground connected again, forming an enormous electromagnetic field.

A disaster that covered tens of meters of land.

“……!!”

Ihyeon couldn’t even manage a gasp of awe. He simply stood there, dazed, watching the overwhelming scene unfold.


[You have caught a glimpse of ‘Zeus’s Condensed Thunder’!]

[You have grasped the concept of condensation.]

[You have gained a new perspective on explosions.]

[You have realized a new approach to Thunder Demon (Noegwi).]

……

[Your understanding of the lightning attribute has deepened. Your analysis of Dueokshini’s Breath (C) has become more profound.]

[The skill proficiency of Dueokshini’s Breath (C) has greatly increased.]

[You have drawn a little closer to the myth of Zeus!]


Just witnessing it was enough to raise his skill proficiency. That was how much inspiration Ihyeon received from Sun Wukong’s strike.

A new world he had never seen even during his time as Jet existed right here.

This was the power of a god.

Or a demon.

Or a giant.

Or perhaps… a dragon.

Beings so transcendent that their very existence completed their own <Narrative> and allowed them to share their <Story> with the world—

That was a power only those of <Logos> could wield.

「Someday, you’ll have to become strong enough to produce lightning of this level. But for now, it’s better to focus on the basics, right?」

Sun Wukong shrugged as if it were nothing, but when he saw the respect shining in Ihyeon’s eyes, he smiled in satisfaction.

「Now then, I’ll show you the technique that uses Condensed Thunder, Diovis (Diowiseu).」
