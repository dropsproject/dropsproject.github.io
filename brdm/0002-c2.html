Chapter 2: Pathfinder Jang Ihyeon (2)

It was no coincidence that Ihyeon had found the Monkey Den.

‘My brother once mentioned that it didn’t seem like the monkeys had simply given him the Fiery Golden Eyes as a gift.’


—You passed their qualification test, so you’re saying you deserve to have this power or something like that?

—That might be so?

—Hyung.

—Yeah.

—Stop reading so many comics. Live in reality.

—……You little punk?

—You’re not even a middle schooler, yet you go, “I’m the chosen one, kukuk.” Seriously?

—You wanna die?


Back then, he brushed it off playfully, but Ihyeon had never actually taken Mun Suchan’s words lightly.

Because that information might prove useful to Mun Suchan someday.

And from that—

Ihyeon and Mun Suchan drew a few conclusions based on those memories.


1. The monkeys must have had a specific reason for giving Mun Suchan the Fiery Golden Eyes.

2. That reason is likely connected to the original owner of the Fiery Golden Eyes, in other words, Sun Wukong.

3. As long as he possesses the Fiery Golden Eyes, the chances of meeting Sun Wukong someday will increase.

4. Sun Wukong may not be as far away as one might think. There is a strong possibility that he is in Korea.


So Ihyeon gathered and thoroughly analyzed every legend and myth about Sun Wukong found throughout the world.

Because of that—

He was confident he could perfectly clear any dungeon related to Sun Wukong.

That was the very reason he managed to pass through the Monkey Den without taking any damage.

And above all—

He possessed the perfect set of “eyes” for it.


[Fiery Golden Eyes (F)]

Eyes said to have been acquired by the Great Sage, Heaven’s Equal, Sun Wukong, after narrowly escaping the flames of the Eight Trigrams Furnace.

The higher the skill mastery, the more hidden truths of the world can be perceived.

—Type: Sacred Relic, Divine Authority

—Origin: Myth “Journey to the West”

*Due to the inability to use mana, the grade has been drastically reduced. Only limited functions (danger detection, riddle solving) are available.


With only the Fiery Golden Eyes, Moon Suchan had risen to become not just a member of the Blue Thunder Unit but one of the world’s greatest scouts.

Even without any mana, the Fiery Golden Eyes was still the Fiery Golden Eyes.

Moreover—


—These eyes… if you were the one to have them, I think they’d suit you perfectly. If you combined them with your unique traits, you could probably devour everything in your path, you know?


Mun Suchan had often said for a long time that the Fiery Golden Eyes would suit Ihyeon perfectly.

At the time, Ihyeon had thought, ‘This hyung of mine just doesn’t want to work and is dumping everything on me.’

But looking back now, it was clear that Mun Suchan had seen something even then.

“……”

That was why—

Ihyeon vowed that this time, he would find a clue related to the “original owner,” no matter what.

Clench.

His fist tightened with determination.


***


A few hours later.

Kukukukung!

The two trees that had tightly blocked the path like a door collapsed.


[You have entered the boss room.]

[This place is filled with countless riddles. Solve them all to meet the hidden master.]


“It’s the boss room!”

“What? We already made it?”

“So this is where it was hiding? No wonder we couldn’t find it until now…!”

The party gasped in awe as they discovered a new clearing hidden deep within the jungle.

The closer they got to the center, the stronger the resistance from the zombie monkeys became, and everyone was showing signs of fatigue. Even so, their faces were bright with smiles.

Not only had they opened the boss room that no one had been able to access for the past year—

“This looks like a temple, doesn’t it?”

“Legendary? Mythical? Whatever it is, ha! It’s a jackpot!”

“Haha! Great work, everyone.”

The clearing itself was massive in scale.

Along the outer edges, stone tablets lined like walls were covered with murals depicting monkeys at war, and at the center, a bronze brazier spewed flames.

Around the brazier, eight giant monkey statues stood as if guarding it.

To anyone who looked at it, it was unmistakably the ritual site of some primitive tribe.

“There are monkeys everywhere here too.”

“Which monkey gods are there again?”

“Hanuman?”

“There’s also Sun Wukong!”

“Sun Wukong’s more of a trickster than a god though. Maybe something like Huhga or Mashu?”

“Whatever it is, it’s definitely a huge find.”

A temple based on mythology was worth as much as anyone was willing to pay.

However—

Even after such an achievement, Ihyeon wasn’t showing off. He was instead carefully inspecting every part of the boss room.

“What are you looking around for like that? You look like someone searching for something.”

Im Wonyoung approached Ihyeon’s side.

Ihyeon gave her a slight nod.

“The raid isn’t completely over yet. I was checking if there might be other traps.”

“It’s a riddle quest, isn’t it? Then we might as well take a short break first.”

“We can’t know how it’ll appear.”

A blunt reply.

Im Wonyoung let out a hollow laugh.

“Hunter Jang, you might have been called a workaholic back when you were in Blue Thunder, right?”

Ihyeon silently stared at her.

“Don’t get mad at Assistant Manager Min. I asked him a few questions since I was curious about you. I had a lot of friends in Blue Thunder too.”

“……”

“Have you ever thought about joining White Burn?”

Ihyeon shook his head.

“I’m sorry. With a body like this, I’d only be a burden.”

“Did you eat something that made you so firm-minded? You answer too fast. Don’t be like that, take your time to think. We can help with your curse treatment as part of the agreement, and we’ll provide other support if you need it…”

“I’m sorry.”

“You really must have eaten something to make you this firm.”

Im Wonyoung laughed dryly.

But the longer she looked at Ihyeon’s steady, unyielding eyes, the more drawn she felt to him.

‘So this is really the man behind the Gangneung Incident? The monster genius of the 17th generation.’

In truth, even Im Wonyoung could never forget that great incident from four years ago.

After all, many of her own friends and classmates had died then.

The Gangneung Incident. The so-called <Ice Palace> dungeon was still spoken of like a legend in the Hunter world.

A 4-star dungeon, once a break occurs, could trigger a transnational catastrophe of unimaginable scale.

So when the Ice Palace appeared right in the middle of Jeongdongjin, not only the citizens of Gangneung but also residents along the entire East Coast were ordered to evacuate.

The government issued an emergency mobilization to every guild in Korea, and even sent requests for aid abroad, focusing all efforts on creating a massive raid force.

‘That was when the Blue Thunder Unit was dispatched.’

Their mission was to delay the break for as long as possible until the main force arrived.

However—

‘The problem was that, contrary to the government’s predictions, the break started progressing much faster than expected.’

If left alone, it would have turned into a large-scale disaster, so the Blue Thunder Unit had no choice but to attempt the raid themselves.

And so—

After paying a tremendous price, they finally succeeded in the raid.

The spotlight that the Blue Thunder Unit received from domestic and international media at the time was immense.

Heroes who saved Gangneung without significant civilian casualties.

In particular, the agent known to have led them, code-named “Jet,” was even treated with near-religious reverence.

However, his true identity was never revealed, and since his whereabouts were never reported to the media afterward, he was naturally forgotten over time…

‘It wasn’t that the military tried to bury it. He actually retired due to the aftereffects, so there were no rumors to spread.’

With a career like that, it seemed likely that various organizations would have tried to recruit him—

But Assistant Manager Min said he didn’t know the exact reason why Ihyeon chose to become a civilian.

Neither he nor even his fellow recruits did.

Apparently, everyone lost contact with him as soon as he was discharged.

‘There must have been a reason. A reason he couldn’t stay in the military.’

If Assistant Manager Min’s words were true, it meant Ihyeon had worked as a pathfinder for a full four years while suffering from both depleted mana and a leg that couldn’t function properly.

That alone showed his incredible mental strength.

How much blood and sweat must he have shed to overcome those two fatal flaws for a Hunter?

Im Wonyoung wanted to grant almost any request Ihyeon might make.

But Ihyeon cut her off sharply before even hearing the full offer.

It was enough to make Im Wonyoung’s face burn with embarrassment.

“Why not just…!”

She still tried to persuade him somehow—

Flash!

‘…What was that?’

In that instant, Im Wonyoung saw Ihyeon’s eyes shine brightly in gold.

And then—

“Team Leader.”

“Yes?”

“It seems things are about to get dangerous. This place isn’t a temple.”

“…What did you say?”

The sudden words from Ihyeon froze Im Wonyoung’s expression.


***


Ihyeon opened his fiery golden eyes as wide as he could and examined the boss room more closely.

For a moment, the pain was so intense it felt as if his eyeballs might burst, but now was not the time to care about that.

‘Statues covering their eyes, noses, and mouths with their hands, murals depicting myths without gods, braziers burning even without offerings……’

He had originally been observing the place to see if he could find any clues related to Sun Wukong—

Yet, strangely enough, the boss room was filled with contradictions, making it hard to believe it was a temple.

Even though gods, by nature, are supposed to be perfect beings.

Which meant...?

“This place is…!”

Just as Ihyeon was about to speak, his eyes suddenly stung with pain.

It was a reaction that appeared whenever an unexpected situation occurred.

He quickly turned his gaze in another direction, and—

Thwack!

In that instant, he saw a hunter lingering near the doorway get struck by an iron arrow, his head shattered.


[The riddle has begun.]

[From this point on, gather the scattered clues and defeat the monkeys.]


Following Ihyeon’s gaze, Im Wonyoung shouted urgently.

“Everyone, battle formation!”

However—

Bwoo-oo-oo!

Suddenly, the sound of a horn echoed along the ceiling.

An echo rippled through the brush-covered area.

And then—

Doom! Doom! Doom! Doom...!

The moment they heard the war drums signaling the beginning of battle, a chill of tension ran down the Hunters’ spines.

“Everyone, lower your heads!”

Ihyeon saw the murals begin to move strangely and shouted in haste.

Fyusususuk!

Then, countless steel arrows rained down from the ceiling like a torrential shower.

“Tower Shield!”

Assistant Manager Min and the other tankers rushed forward, stacking their Tower Shields one over another like a fortress wall.

Tadadadang!

Fortunately, most of the steel arrows failed to pierce through and bounced off, but the force behind them was strong enough that several tankers lost their balance and staggered.

The Hunters who hadn’t managed to hide behind the Tower Shields in time ran desperately in search of cover.

“Thank you, Team Leader.”

“No, it’s our fault for bringing Hunter Jang into such a dangerous area. Damn it.”

Thanks to Im Wonyoung’s quick reaction, Ihyeon barely avoided the barrage by taking cover behind a pillar jutting out from the corner, but two more Hunters were killed in the chaos.

Im Wonyoung clenched her teeth, veins bulging around her eyes.

She quickly pulled a cigarette from her pocket and put it to her lips. It wasn’t to act calm. She needed it to activate a skill.

As soon as the cigarette was lit, smoke spread thickly through the air like fog.

Goooooo!


[‘Legacy Skill: Back When Tigers Smoked Cigarettes’ has been activated. Two Jangsanbeoms are summoned.]


Two white, long-furred tigers stood tall, baring their teeth.

Kreuhhh!

As the two Jangsanbeoms charged into the boss room, they tried to destroy the traps—

But the problem was that the traps didn’t end there.

Dudududu!

With the pounding sound of hooves striking the ground, ancient chariots burst out from the murals.

Led by three horses each, monkey warriors riding the chariots struck the Jangsanbeoms one after another and bound their ankles.

Bang! Bang!

The remaining two ancient chariots made a wide turn and crashed directly into the tower shields.

“B-block them!”

But the formation collapsed far too easily.

Worse, monkey soldiers began pouring out from the murals behind them, mercilessly attacking the hunters.

As expected, they were too heavily armored and well-armed, making them difficult to deal with.

“U-ugh! Gah!”

“Why are there so many… Kuaaagh!”

“Everyone, snap out of it! Fall back as much as you can!”

Although all the hunters of White Burn were elite, the monkey soldiers were far more vicious and cunning.

Not only did they move in groups of four, hunting the hunters one by one, but whenever someone tried to counterattack, they would leap into the trees and vanish, making skills useless.

In the end, the group of over thirty people was cut down to nearly half in an instant.

At this rate, they would be annihilated.

“You bastards!”

Im Wonyoung unleashed every skill she had to take down the monsters, but it wasn’t enough to turn the tide.

Unlike their small elite unit, the enemy was a full-scale army.

At this level, it didn’t seem like a 2-star difficulty anymore, but 3-star. No, maybe even higher.

‘I have to find a way somehow…!’

At that moment, Im Wonyoung saw Ihyeon suddenly sit on the ground and unfasten the military pack he had been carrying.

What was he trying to do in a situation this urgent?

But Ihyeon remained entirely focused on what he was doing. His eyes now radiated a brilliant golden light, shining like the sun.

Paaaah!


[You have used the ‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ beyond the permitted limit. Your mental strength can no longer endure it.]

[Warning! If the sacred relic overheats beyond its limit, your mental barrier may weaken, and you may become corrupted by its power.]

[Warning!]

……


[Accelerating calculations based on the discovered information. Searching for an escape route.]


As if he were a soldier standing guard, he scanned his surroundings without rest, then pulled something out of his bag.

‘…A gun?’

It was an HK416, the same model often used for training at the academy.

Clack! Clack!

Ihyeon assembled the firearm’s parts in an instant and even attached the sight.

That was not all.

On his left belt hung a KA-BAR combat knife and a kukri, while on the right were flashbangs, smoke grenades, and hand grenades.

He looked every bit like a soldier heading to war.

‘So that’s why he brought a military pack!’

Clack!

The sound of the final slide being pulled snapped Im Wonyoung out of her brief daze.

In that instant, she realized.

‘This dude, could it be that he’s been waiting for this moment?’

Whether by design or coincidence, she could not tell—

But the emotion flickering across Ihyeon’s eyes was unmistakably exhilaration.

Indeed, he was truly delighted.

‘These phenomena. I’m certain. We’ve reached the final gate leading to where Sun Wukong is.’

It was exactly as Mun Suchan had said.

A long-awaited opportunity that had finally arrived after four years.

He could not possibly let it slip away.

Ihyeon’s head throbbed as if it would split open, but he turned toward Im Wonyoung with the Fiery Golden Eyes shining more brightly than ever.

He needed her help to pass through the gate.

However—

“Hunter Jang, do you have a plan?”

Im Wonyoung asked first, her face serious.

Ihyeon gave a heavy nod.

“Yes. I do. But it’s more of a personal method.”

“Still, I think you have far more experience in this than I do, don’t you, Hunter Jang?”

After a long breath, Im Wonyoung held out a brooch, a communication item.

“If I hand over command authority to you, what do you think? Can we get out of here?”

“……”

Ihyeon glanced between the brooch and Im Wonyoung with an indifferent look for a brief moment.

A trace of tension crossed her face.

“If it ends with me only getting out, there are a lot of people who will curse me.”

“…?”

“At the very least, we have to clear it.”

“…!”

Im Wonyoung’s face brightened.
