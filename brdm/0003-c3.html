Chapter 3: Pathfinder Jang Ihyeon (3)

The raid party was overjoyed the moment they saw that the boss room was decorated like a temple.

Since unearthing a sacred relic imbued with divine power was an incredible career achievement.

And if they were lucky enough to be chosen by the relic?

They could even turn their lives around.

However—

Ihyeon could tell that it was all a trick deliberately crafted by the room’s creator.

‘This isn’t a temple, but a tomb, a royal tomb where a dead king is buried.’

There were three clues that led him to that conclusion.


[‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ has uncovered the <Riddle> hidden in this place using the clues identified by its owner, successfully finding part of the answer.]


[1. The Eight Statues Surrounding the Altar]

[The eight statues appear to be offering worship to a god, but in truth, they are in a peculiar posture, bowing with their eyes, nose, and mouth covered by both hands.]

[This signifies that the king has departed to a place where he cannot be seen, spoken to, or heard, and is therefore longed for.]

……


[2. Mural Without a God]

[At first glance, the mural appears to depict deeds honoring a god. However, a closer look reveals that it portrays no one.]

[This signifies resurrection, suggesting that he will soon return to guide them as he once did.]

[Since death does not exist for a god, it means that the master of this place is a mortal king.]

……


[3. Brazier Without an Offering]

[Beside the altar, where no offering has been placed, a brazier burns brightly.]

[This can be seen as a sign of reverence for the king, expressing a wish for his eternal life and his eventual return.]

……


Solving a riddle is considered a remarkable achievement.

It signifies that one has ‘understood’ the master of the dungeon.

Therefore, the greater the master’s stature, the greater the reward received.


[You have glimpsed a part of the secret hidden within <Monkey Den>.]

[You have interpreted the myth (shinhwa) of ■■■■. You gain a portion of its karma (eob).]

[Your rank has increased.]

[Your rank has increased.]

……

[It has been confirmed that the karma of ■■■■ is deeply connected to the ‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’.]

[All the karma of ■■■■ you obtained has been completely absorbed into your body.]


Thud! Thud-thud-thud!

Ihyeon’s body had been undergoing constant changes for some time now.

The mana circuits that had been frozen by the curse were gradually loosening, and strength was returning to his once-damaged muscles.

A refreshing sensation he hadn’t felt in four years.

Though his mana hadn’t fully recovered, just regaining a bit of vitality was already an incredible feat.

‘With this much… I can at least run for a while.’


[You have successfully lifted a portion of the curse that bound your body!]


This was proof that the dungeon was deeply connected to the true holder of Fiery Golden Eyes, Sun Wukong.

That was why he had to solve the boss room’s riddle and meet the “hidden master,” Sun Wukong, no matter what.

‘If all these monkeys are the ones who followed Sun Wukong on Mount Huaguo in Journey to the West, and if this royal tomb itself is where Sun Wukong is buried, then it’s entirely plausible.’

If there was any uncertainty, it was about why Sun Wukong, who had clearly become a Buddha in Journey to the West, would have a “tomb” at all……

‘Let’s think about that part later.’

Organizing his thoughts, Ihyeon spoke to Im Wonyoung.

“Alright. I’ll accept your proposal, Team Leader. But I have one condition.”

“Just speak.”

“Until we clear the boss room, you must follow my orders without question.”

Im Wonyoung nodded.

“Of course. Don’t worry.”

“Then I’ll find a way to save the remaining people somehow.”

If anyone else had said that, it would have sounded like empty bravado.

But when Ihyeon said it, it already felt as if it had been done.

“Please, I’m counting on you.”

Ihyeon felt that Im Wonyoung was cut from a different cloth.

Even in a desperate situation, it was human nature to cling to one’s authority.

Yet Im Wonyoung was calm enough to assess the situation and bold enough to entrust the task to someone she deemed more capable than herself.

A temperament completely unlike his own, who had lived his whole life rough like a fighting dog.

It seemed that White Burn had a bright future ahead of them.

“Then, please hand it over. The brooch.”

Ihyeon took the brooch that Im Wonyoung handed him.

It was shaped like a donkey’s ear.


[The King’s Ears in the Bamboo Forest]

If you whisper a secret you do not wish to tell others into the bamboo forest, that secret will be heard only by those you want to hear it.

—Type: Miscellaneous item.

—Origin: Fairy tale ‘The King’s Ears Are Donkey Ears’


A communicator that every member of White Burn carries.

After briefly hearing how to use it, Ihyeon brought his lips close to the brooch.

『Ah, ah, can everyone hear my voice?』

The hunters scattered throughout the boss room all turned their attention toward where Ihyeon and Im Wonyoung were.

Only then did they realize that Ihyeon was armed with a firearm.

『……We hear you.』

『I can hear you.』

『I can hear you.』

Ihyeon then spoke.

『I have just received command authority from the team leader. From this point on, you will follow my instructions. Even a moment’s hesitation could lead to the death of your comrades. Responses will be unified as “Command acknowledged.”』

The hunters looked to Im Wonyoung.

She nodded, signaling them to comply.

Soon, replies came back.

『Command acknowledged.』

『Command acknowledged.』

『Then, given the situation, I’ll skip formalities from now on.』

At that moment, old memories flashed through Ihyeon’s mind.

Four years ago, when he was on a mission with his own team.

『If you look around, you should see white cloths hanging here and there.』

The hunters quickly surveyed their surroundings.

Indeed, white cloths were tied around stone monuments and statues in several places.

『When I give the signal, run with all your strength to where the white cloths are. Then cover your head with the cloth.』

Signal?

As confusion spread across the hunters’ faces—

Ihyeon threw a flash grenade hanging from his right belt high into the air.

Fweee!

“……!”

“……!!”

The whole world turned completely white.

The hunters felt as if they might go blind for an instant, but without hesitation, each dashed toward their marked spots.

Tap!

Their movements were made possible because they had experienced various curses during group training at White Burn.

In contrast, the monkeys were different.

Ukikki!

Uki!

Since it was their first time seeing such a flash, they couldn’t help but panic—


[With the acquired karma from ■■■■, the ‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ undergoes transformation. A new function has been added.]

[Additional Function: Weakness Detection.]

[‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ begins identifying the enemies’ weaknesses!]


Ihyeon was aiming for that exact moment.

Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!

Bullets, crafted by his own hand and mixed with silver, pierced through the foreheads of the monkey soldiers.

As long as he struck only the weak points marked by Fiery Golden Eyes, he could easily fulfill the role of a single hunter.

Of course, his standard for what counted as “a single hunter’s worth” came from the Blue Thunder Unit.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

The muzzle flashed without rest. Even with a silencer attached, each blast was followed by the sound of another monkey soldier’s head exploding in succession.

‘The power of firearms is this strong!?’

‘W-what kind of magic did he use...?’

The hunters gaped as they ran forward, watching the monkey soldiers collapse one after another.

Most hunters preferred cold weapons since they were easier to imbue with mana—

But Ihyeon was overturning that common sense far too openly.

Baaang!

Meanwhile, the hunters reached their destination with minimal damage—

And the moment they tore the white cloth and draped it over their heads as instructed, they understood why Ihyeon had given such an order.


[White Cloth Marked with a Swastika]

It carries a ritual meaning of warding off evil spirits. At times, it is also used to cover a corpse during transport.

The moment you drape it over yourself, you are regarded as dead.


'Regarded as a dead person……!’

‘Then, wouldn’t that mean this place isn’t a temple, but a tomb?’

‘Which means these monkeys are like grave keepers, protecting the tomb……’

‘Wait, if that’s the case?’

The experienced hunters turned to look at the monkey soldiers who had been chasing them.

Then the monkey soldiers faltered.

They seemed confused, as if they suddenly couldn’t perceive the hunters.

‘They can’t find us!’

Bang!

Another monkey soldier’s head burst apart.

‘How on earth did he figure that out!?’

‘In such a short time……!’

Ihyeon wasn’t just skilled at finding pathways. He possessed the intuition and insight to find the ‘right answer.’


[Congratulations! You have successfully solved the first riddle.]

[From now on, you are priests assisting in the royal tomb’s ritual. Complete the ritual.]


A phrase found in the message.

‘Ritual!’

The secret of the boss room was finally revealed.

At the moment when everyone’s eyes widened—

『If you’ve put on the white cloth, then next……!』

An additional command was heard.

They were ordered to form teams of three and move to their designated positions.

Just as the hunters were about to perk up their ears—

Ukkkiii!

Along with the furious cries of monkeys, five ancient chariots charged forward.

Dudududu—

They were targeting Ihyeon, who had yet to cover himself with a white cloth.

It was as if they would not let the cause of this chaos go unpunished.

『Careful……!』

『Do not leave your assigned position. I repeat, do not leave your assigned position.』

The hunters flinched, but Ihyeon remained calm.

His eyes now glowed completely gold, with no distinction between iris and sclera.

It was proof that the karma of Sun Wukong, gained by solving the riddle, was continuously evolving the Fiery Golden Eyes.


[You have achieved 98% strategy contribution for the first riddle. You have obtained a significant portion of ■■■■’s karma.]

[Some of the locked functions of the ‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ have been unlocked. Its performance has greatly improved.]

[Your body movements have become slightly more agile!]


Ukki-kkki!

Just then, one of the chariots charged toward him. It looked as if it intended to trample Ihyeon flat under its hooves.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

But Ihyeon calmly pulled the trigger—

And the three horse heads exploded one after another, flipping the cart over.

Right at that moment, Ihyeon moved. He tossed his firearm far away onto the ground and leapt high into the air.

The bodies of the horses and the overturned cart swept past the spot where he had been just a second ago. Ihyeon flipped over them and landed in front of a monkey warrior.

Ukkiiii!

The monkey warrior thrust its sword toward him with a furious face—

And as soon as Ihyeon descended, he drew the kukri from his left belt with his right hand and slashed downward.

It was a movement so agile it was almost acrobatic.

Slash!

The kukri not only sliced through the monkey warrior’s sword but also cut diagonally across its nape in a single stroke.

It was the result of precisely striking the weak point marked by the Fiery Golden Eyes.

Fwoooosh!

The warrior’s head flew off, and blood spurted like a fountain.

At the same time, Ihyeon brought his left hand to the Glock 26 holstered under his shoulder.

Thud! Thud! Thud!

The eyeballs of the other monkey warriors burst one after another.

No matter how tough a monster’s hide was, its eyes could never be as resilient.

In an instant, the chariots lost their masters and overturned one after another, plunging the area into chaos.

“……!”

“……!”

“……!”

It was an unbelievable scene.

The hunters could only gape in shock.

‘Is that… even possible…?’

‘No way, can someone without magic even move like that? And with one leg not even fully healed… what kind of training does that take…!?’

Even Im Wonyoung, who had asked him to take command, was equally astonished.

Even she, as a ranker, couldn’t be sure she could display such overwhelming skill.

‘So this is the last leader of Blue Thunder, Jet…!’

Without realizing it, Im Wonyoung clenched her fist.

Thump! Thump! Thump!

Whether it was from witnessing a living legend before her eyes or from the heat of the battlefield—

For some reason her heart pounded uncontrollably. Her face flushed red.

The other hunters felt the same.

Ihyeon approached the nearest chariot.

There, too, a white cloth was tied. But this one was stained with the dull red of blood and dust from the dead warrior.

Ihyeon untied it and draped it over his shoulder.

Flap!

The wind blew.


[You are now a priest who presides over the ritual. Complete the ritual and meet the hidden master.]


“……”

“……”

“……”

Ihyeon’s suffocating presence filled the boss room.

‘In here. Right at this very spot—’

Ihyeon’s golden eyes gleamed.

‘I will summon Sun Wukong.’
