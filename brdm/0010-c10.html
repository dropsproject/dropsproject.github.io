Chapter 10: Temporary Apostle (5)

Ihyeon resumed his forced march.

As he recalled the lessons he had learned from Sun Wukong, he focused on finishing his exploration of the dungeon as quickly as possible.

‘If I take too long, brother Jin-u will start nagging me again.’

There was nothing scarier than a doctor’s nagging.


[You have crossed more than half of the desert. You are now entering a new area.]

[Progress rate: 62%]


Crackle! Crack-crack-crackle!

Bluish-white lightning flashed relentlessly.

The output of his lightning energy, now risen to Grade C, was incomparable to what it had been at the start. It was even capable of striking targets from long range.

‘After releasing it all…’

Because of that, bluish-white flashes streaked across the sky several times.


[You have defeated the Fire-tailed Bat.]

[You have defeated the Burning Bird.]

……


‘Draw it in.’

He attempted to control and retrieve the lightning energy that had scattered in all directions.

In the past, he would have simply released it and generated new lightning energy again.

But ever since learning the concept of Condensed Thunder, that approach felt far too inefficient.

Thanks to that, as some of the discharged lightning energy returned to him, the radiance grew even brighter—

‘And release again.’

Kwakwa-kwang!

The area was once more engulfed by lightning as it burst forth again, its power even greater than before.


[You have eliminated a large number of Yellow Wolves.]

[You have discovered and destroyed the nest of a Hidden Giant Scorpion.]

[Strength has increased by 1.]

……

[Your control over mana has improved. You are gradually understanding the concept of Condensed Thunder.]

[Intelligence has increased by 1.]


Applying the concept of Condensed Thunder to lightning energy made its power more stable and its mana consumption lower, which pleased him—

But there were still quite a few issues that required attention.

‘Maybe because it’s energy that has already been released once, it’s too difficult to retrieve it.’

To properly activate Diovis, it required the prerequisite of complete control over lightning energy.

However, once the mana was released outward, it was effectively out of his hands, and the recovery rate became abysmal.

‘I need another method. Even if it’s a shortcut, something that lets me freely control the lightning energy.’

Ihyeon glanced toward Sun Wukong for a moment, but he only smiled innocently and cheerfully.

「Why? Got something to ask? Go ahead, ask anything.」

“No, nothing at all.”

Ihyeon calmly shook his head.

This was not a problem Sun Wukong could solve for him. It was an answer he had to find on his own.

Then, a thought flashed through his mind.

‘What if I expand the limit of Dueokshini’s Breath as much as possible, and unfold Diovis through Dueokshini’s Breath?’

Pazizizik!!


[Skill Proficiency of Dueokshini’s Breath (C) has increased.]

……


How much time had passed like that?


[Most of the desert has been traversed. Now entering the final area.]

[Progress rate: 81%]


By the time he entered a new area—

The nature of the wind blowing through the desert had begun to change.

If it had only been hot before, now it felt as if it was stealing his breath away.

「The wind has gotten really hot, hasn’t it?」

“Yes. It seems to have become much fiercer. My stamina keeps dropping too.”

Ihyeon narrowed his eyes as he felt his already limited stamina being drained.

‘There has to be some kind of secret in the wind.’

No, it wasn’t just the wind.

There was definitely a secret hidden in the underground water veins that had guided Ihyeon all this time.

‘The wind, the desert, the water veins……? I see. This is the Desert of the Yellow Wind Fiend.’

When he arranged the keywords in order, a certain place from Journey to the West immediately came to mind.

No, rather, it was one of Sun Wukong’s enemies.

Dding!


[‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ has gathered all clues and revealed the hidden form of <Yellow Desert>!]

[The gateway leading to ‘Yellow Wind Ridge, where the Divine Wind of Samadhi blows,’ located at the edge of the desert, has opened.]

[You have deciphered the myth of ■■■■. You have gained a portion of his karma.]

[Your rank has increased.]


‘As expected.’

Ihyeon’s eyes gleamed.

This place was not just an ordinary desert.

He hadn’t realized it at first because of how vast the area was, and because the sand beneath his feet swallowed his steps with every move.

In truth, it was a canyon region with a considerable slope. In other words, a mountain.

‘So this is where the Divine Wind of Samadhi blows?’

However, rather than the dungeon’s true nature, Ihyeon’s attention was drawn to one particular line in the message.

「What the hell? No wonder the wind felt so damn nasty. So this was the path leading to Yellow Wind Ridge?」

Sun Wukong’s expression was openly filled with disgust.

「Hey, the owner of this place isn’t just some simpleton, you know? It’s very possible they already realized you were coming and made preparations. So be careful.」

“I should make preparations as well, then.”

「You think that’ll help? With your current strength, you’re not even on par with that bastard’s toenail.」

Sun Wukong grinned.

As if he were dying to see what kind of reaction Ihyeon would show.

But then—

“If it were the real master of Yellow Wind Ridge, that would be true. But the one over there isn’t the real one, is it?”

「...Tch, boring.」

“If it were that dangerous, I would have already been struggling to survive the moment I entered this dungeon.”

It seemed that the difficulty of the instance dungeon selected by the Fiery Golden Eyes was adjusted according to Ihyeon’s current ability.

If it wasn’t, he would have died instantly before he could take any measures.

「Still, you shouldn’t take it lightly. The Divine Wind of Samadhi isn’t some easy technique to deal with.」

Ihyeon quietly nodded.

Just because it was within his limits didn’t mean it would be easy.

Besides, the fact that Sun Wukong specifically pointed it out meant it was truly dangerous.

It was right then, just as Ihyeon was about to speak.


[‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ has detected danger!]


Paat!

Suddenly, Ihyeon’s eyes turned cold, and he vanished from his spot.

Then—

Bang!

He reappeared a short distance away and slammed something into the ground.

“W-wait! Stop! Stooop!”

He was about to swing his axe down to sever the neck of the one spying on him when a desperate voice speaking Korean echoed out.

Ihyeon’s hand stopped midair.

“S-save me! I-I wasn’t trying to p-peep or launch a s-surprise attack, n-not at all!”

Beneath the dented helmet was the face of a slender woman.

A human.

And a hunter at that.


***


“Um, I know you're just being cautious, but… could you please loosen this a little?”

The woman who had introduced herself as “Kim Se-jeong” gave an awkward smile as she looked at the binding rope tightly wrapped around her wrists.

However—

Swiik!

Instead of answering, Ihyeon raised his axe toward her.

“...N-no, never mind. That was careless of me. P-please, do as you see fit.”

Kim Se-jeong lowered her gaze slightly.

Ihyeon’s expression appeared cold. It was the result of his training during his Blue Thunder days, which taught him to keep his emotions unreadable even in front of hostage-takers.

“I-if I could just have a sip of water instead…”

Thud.

“Th-thank you!”

Kim Se-jeong hurriedly opened the cap of the plastic bottle Ihyeon had tossed her and gulped down the water in haste.

Her dehydration must have been that severe as she even choked a little, yet she never spat any of it out.

‘Great Sage.’

While watching her, Ihyeon called out to Sun Wukong.

「What?」

‘Wasn’t this supposed to be an instance dungeon? Then why is she…?’

「You mean another person besides you?」

‘Yes.’

「Well, I don’t know either.」

‘……?’

Ihyeon wondered if Sun Wukong was joking even now and turned to look at him.

Sun Wukong only shrugged.

「Looks like she entered through a [Temporary Gate] that appeared due to some unknown error? Otherwise—」

At that moment, the corner of Sun Wukong’s lips curled upward.

「She could be some fool chasing after my myth?」

‘……!’

An unexpected twist.

「If you think you could monopolize my myth, you’d better fix that notion right now. I am the Great Sage, Heaven’s Equal, once the king of all demons, and also the Battle Buddha who attained divinity through endless asceticism. Do you really think you’re the only one after my myth?」

‘…….’

「I’d say it’s quite possible that the main gods who turned me into this state, and maybe a few others who noticed my condition, wouldn’t find it strange to go seeking my legends themselves.」

Ihyeon thought Sun Wukong’s golden eyes blazed like flames.

「Even if they can’t easily descend to the mortal realm, they could still move their apostles and search for my legends, right?」

Ihyeon could only nod.

It made sense. Back in White Burn, they had tried to continue exploring the <Monkey Den> without forcibly raising its level.

They might not have uncovered Sun Wukong’s secret, but they could have at least sensed that something was hidden there.

‘Then could that woman also be tied to the Great Sage’s myth?’

「Well, who really knows. I don’t sense any particular aura from her right now, but there could be some connection. Or maybe she just got caught up in it.」

Sun Wukong shrugged again, but Ihyeon couldn’t shake the feeling that it was the former.

Otherwise, there was no way she could have entered a dungeon whose gate wasn’t even fully materialized yet.

Besides—

‘For someone who just happened to get dragged in, her equipment is far too complete.’

It was proof that she had entered the dungeon intentionally and fully prepared.

She simply seemed to have failed in her raid attempt.

Her armor was nearly torn to shreds and on the verge of breaking, and her weapon’s edge was completely chipped.

‘I need to figure out what happened to her inside first.’

The woman possessed exceptional stealth abilities, sharp enough to deceive even Ihyeon’s senses.

Had she not been exhausted, or if Ihyeon hadn’t fully activated Fiery Golden Eyes’s insight, he might not have detected her presence at all.

If she had ambushed him, things could have turned disastrous. So, it was only right to examine the situation carefully.

‘Especially that stealth skill she used. She suppressed her mana flow as much as possible to hide her presence… perhaps…!’

Ihyeon did not overlook the fact that the secret to her stealth skill lay in the ring on her left index finger.


[Using ‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ to analyze the ring worn by the opponent.]

……

[Analyzing.]

……

[The ring has been identified as ‘Footsteps of the Dusky Shadow’. It condenses mana, allowing the wearer to rest within shadows, and……]

……

[You have obtained a minor clue about condensation.]


‘As I thought.’

Ihyeon forcibly suppressed a smile that had almost burst out without his noticing.

“Did you say your name is Kim Se-jeong?”

Kim Se-jeong, who was about to drink the last of the water in the empty plastic bottle, looked at Ihyeon in surprise.

“Yes…….”

“May I ask what happened when you entered?”

Kim Se-jeong hesitated for a moment before answering and rolled her eyes anxiously.

‘If I try to clear this dungeon while this woman and her party are here, there is a 100 percent chance they will hinder my raid. I need to find out exactly what they are aiming for and how connected they are to Great Sage’s Myth, then drive them out no matter what.’

Ihyeon had no intention of sharing Sun Wukong’s myth with anyone.

“If you do not want to answer, you do not have to. You must have quenched your thirst, so you may leave now.”

Ihyeon rose from his seat without any hesitation.

At that, Kim Se-jeong flung herself forward and grabbed Ihyeon’s leg in desperation.

“P-please help us! In the desert at the top of the dungeon! My guild members are being held captive! If I leave them like this they might die. So please……!”

For an instant, Ihyeon’s eyes calmed.

If it was the desert at the top, that meant the boss room.

“You know the Prestige Guild, right? We are members there. Among those being held is the guild leader’s younger sibling. So if you help us, you can receive a large compensation!”

Kim Se-jeong, mistaking Ihyeon’s silence for hesitation, tried to persuade him, even showing the badly faded guild mark near her shoulder, where it was barely visible.

At that moment—

‘If I use this situation well, not only will I get clues about the condensation, but strategizing will become easier too.’

Having finished his calculations, Ihyeon asked.

“How many people are being held in the desert in total?”

Ihyeon was a natural born hunter.

A Hunter.

‘If they were chasing the Great Sage’s Myth, I’ll intercept them and send them out. If they try to deceive me or act foolishly because of the myth—’

He knew how to hide his true intentions and seize his desired goal.

‘Eliminate them right there.’

His Fiery Golden Eyes gleamed briefly.
