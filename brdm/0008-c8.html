Chapter 8: Temporary Apostle (3)

‘It really isn’t an easy environment from the start.’

Even for Ihyeon, who prided himself on his wealth of real combat experience, the desert was an extremely difficult place to adapt to.

The sun stung his skin.

The heat was so intense it felt like his flesh was being cooked.

Sandstorms often obscured his vision.

When the wind grew strong, the terrain would constantly shift, rendering any knowledge of the landscape useless.

Because of the heat, his senses were always disturbed, forcing him to rely purely on instinct.

However—

‘That’s why this could be the best place to train my senses.’

Before bringing the [Amulet of Protection] to Im Wonyoung, Ihyeon planned to hone his skills as much as possible.

Because there was something he intended to ask her.

Fwaaht!

The Fiery Golden Eyes pointed somewhere.

A danger signal.

There was no time to even think, ‘Where?’

‘Below!’

Fwaaht!

A wolf burst out from beneath the sand, its jaws opening wide.

It looked like a crocodile snatching prey from a swamp.

‘It can even dig through sand!’

Ihyeon drew two axes and crossed them in an X.

Bang!

Its jaws were blocked in an instant by the axes.

Without a moment’s hesitation, Ihyeon let go of them, drew the kukri hanging from his belt with his left hand, and slashed upward.

Skeuk!

Fwoosh!

Though this kukri wasn’t a custom-made item, it was still crafted by the old master Dochul.

The blade was exceptionally sharp. A deep wound tore across the yellow wolf’s neck, and blood burst forth.


[Strength has increased by 1.]


Kang! Kaekang!

A fountain of blood splattered across the dry desert sand as two figures burst through the sandstorm.

Before Ihyeon could even confirm the increase in his strength coefficient, he hurled his kukri toward one of them.

Perhaps because of the kukri’s large size, its speed was slow, allowing the creature to twist its body and dodge—

But that delay gave Ihyeon just enough of an opening to focus on the other one.

He dashed forward, kicking up the ground beneath his feet.

Before they knew it, the two axes he had dropped earlier were already back in his hands.

Fwoosh!

In that instant, mana surged out from his mana core.

Crackle, crackle—

At the same time, blue-white lightning sprang up along the axes.

For the first time in four years, those two axe blades carried mana again, baring their cruel fangs.

Boom!

Both axes crushed the wolf’s skull and split its jaws wide open.

Just that single exchange.

That was how long it took to slay the yellow wolf with mana pouring out of him after such a long time.

Shhhhk!

Even though the axe blades had clearly cleaved through it, the wounds looked as if they had been chewed by a beast, leaving behind jagged, sawtooth marks.

However—

‘I’m still lacking output.’

Ihyeon did not forget how the recoil had been so strong when he took down the yellow wolf that he had nearly lost his grip on the axes.

If he had released them on purpose, that would be one thing. But losing his weapons just because a monster’s hide was tough would be the worst kind of mistake on the battlefield.

‘I need to pour in more mana.’

With that cool-headed judgment, he stomped hard on the ground once more—

And soon came face to face with the wolf that had dodged his kukri earlier.

‘More!’

Crackle!


[Mana has increased by 2.]


The blue-white lightning, now rougher than before, split through horizontally.

Kang!

The axe struck the wolf’s lower jaw.

However, this wolf seemed stronger than the one before, charging forward unfazed by its wound.

Ihyeon tried to drive the axe in his right hand into the creature’s forehead—

Paat! Pat!

At that moment, three other wolves burst out from the wind and the ground.

He hurriedly swung his right arm to the side. With a crunching sound, one of them died.

But the other two sank their teeth into his left thigh and right flank.

A horrific pain he hadn’t felt in a long time surged through him. Ihyeon’s adrenaline spiked violently.

‘More!!’

And that intense emotion turned into mana.


[‘Trait: Indomitable’ is activated, causing intense emotions to surge. The Dueokshini's Breath (D) releases more mana.]

[Proficiency has significantly increased.]

[Stamina has increased by 1.]

[Mana has increased by 1.]

[Dexterity has increased by 2.]


Kwarrrng!

The lightning energy, which until now had only appeared as simple sparks, had grown strong enough to connect with one another.

At the same time, a rough thunderclap struck down.


[The Dueokshini’s wrath (nogi) manifests!]


The more intensely the Dueokshini’s Breath releases emotion, the greater the mana output it can draw forth.

Fury, agitation, and madness.

As their intensity rose, the power of the blue-white lightning surging from Ihyeon’s two axes grew even stronger.

Two bolts of lightning struck down, shattering the skulls of the yellow wolves blocking his path in an instant.

Puhk! Thud!

Crack!

Covered from head to toe in bright red blood, Ihyeon grinned wide, baring his fangs.

Adrenaline and dopamine surged recklessly.

The pain from his wounds. The frustration of his body failing to move as he wished. Yet an even greater thrill drowned out every imperfection.

Yes.

This was it.

The sensation he had longed to taste.

Clashing head-on, swinging his axes, taking wounds, growing irritated, exhaling hot breaths, and at last, cutting down his enemy.

A feeling that made him vividly aware he was alive.


[Emotions intensify.]

[The Dueokshini’s wrath continues to accelerate the flow of mana. It draws in more of the divine power of ■■■■ that had been lying dormant within the body.]

[Proficiency has greatly increased. The Dueokshini’s Breath (D) has advanced to Grade C.]


“Haah!”

With one long exhale, Ihyeon released all the pent-up resentment and frustration that had built up inside him.

As he drew in another breath, he embedded the tension and exhilaration of the battlefield deep within his body.

Because of that—

Every dulled nerve cell and every dormant motor cell that had lost contact with mana was jolted awake one by one.

Flash!

In that instant, Ihyeon felt as if something tightly sealed within him was awakening once more.


[The Dueokshini's wrath has awakened the long-dormant spirit of the Daeoksini.]

[Senses are being actively heightened.]

[Strength has increased by 2.]

[Agility has increased by 3.]

……

[You have pushed your body beyond its limits. The heightened senses have successfully unlocked the previously sealed skill, ‘Combat Instinct (F)’.]


[Combat Instinct (F)]

A skill possessed only by warriors who have fought fiercely on the battlefield for a long time. The greater the fatigue accumulated in the body, and the more damage sustained, the stronger the fighting spirit grows.

Born from experience, this instinct, intuition, and reflexive judgment exist solely to seek victory. It has no interest in anything else.

—Type: Skill, Combat.

—Proficiency: 0%

—Origin: Traditional tale “Pond of Jangja”


As the second of the five skills Ihyeon had once taken pride in was released—

Kwaaaang!

Ihyeon attempted another dash.

Where the sandstorm had cleared.

There, about twenty yellow wolves were baring their teeth and growling, their eyes gleaming.

At that moment, Ihyeon smiled.

Because there were still plenty of foes left to fight.

And he looked forward to how much more ecstasy they might bring him.

His figure soon appeared as if—

He was a Dueokshini completely mad in battle.

[TL/N: In folklore, a Dueokshini usually appears as a hideous ghost- or ogre-like being with a grotesque face, wild hair, and an aura of rage. It is said to feed on fear or anger, sometimes representing uncontrollable wrath or madness itself.]


***


「Damn it! My fish-shaped pastries! If I knew this would happen, I should’ve just eaten them right away! Aaaagh!」

Sun Wukong, feeling frustrated, tried his best to brush the sand off his fish-shaped pastries—

Fwoooosh!

But each gust of wind only buried them under even more sand.

Eventually, overwhelmed with irritation, Sun Wukong stomped around furiously, then noticed Ihyeon, who was making an even greater commotion than he was.

Kwarung! Kwarurung!

Kwakwakwakwa!

Ihyeon was charging recklessly through the yellow wolves.

He ran as if he were the wolf weaving through a flock of sheep—

But in truth, the one taking the most damage was Ihyeon himself.

Still, he didn’t seem to care at all.


[Pain Resistance (E) has been acquired. You can now endure greater pain.]

[Bleeding Resistance (F) has been acquired. You can now better withstand the effects of blood loss.]


No, he was actually enjoying it even more.

「That brat, he's not doing that on purpose just to raise his resistance, is he?」

If that were true, it would be nothing short of madness.

[Resistance] only manifests when the body is driven to its limits and pushed beyond them.

However, if one could endure such training, it could become a means to overcome various abnormal statuses during battle.

Behind all those perfected forms lay [Diamond Body], which even Sun Wukong had barely achieved……

「Wait, no way, for real? That brat? Did he read my karma……?」

Sun Wukong suddenly felt a chill run down his spine.

The moment he realized it, he knew that Ihyeon’s fighting style looked eerily familiar.

Of course, Ihyeon was also a born fighter.

After all, the very first skills he regained were the Dueokshini’s Breath and Combat Instinct.

But Ihyeon was rebuilding that foundation from the ground up.

The way he used his skills, the way he perceived his surroundings, the way he moved his body, the way he approached battle, the way he reacted to ambushes, the way he channeled mana and infused it into his weapons, and so on.

His entire fighting style!

And—

Beneath it all, faint traces of Sun Wukong’s movements could be seen.

At that moment, Sun Wukong remembered how Ihyeon had observed his mana circulation while he was handling divine power.

「Don’t tell me he never forgot that sensation and kept it all this time? No, he’s been watching me ever since……?」

If he had been observing even Sun Wukong’s smallest habits and finding ways to apply them to himself.

Then those bold yet reckless, but undeniably victorious, movements finally made sense.

Sun Wukong was at a loss for words at the level of obsession and madness Ihyeon possessed.

「……Is he insane, for real.」

That was all Sun Wukong could say.


***


[Pain Resistance (E) has advanced to grade D.]

[Bleeding Resistance (F) has advanced to grade E.]

……

[Paralysis Resistance (F) has been acquired. You have become more resistant to temporary paralysis.]

[Physical Resistance (F) has been acquired. Your muscles and bones have become sturdier.]

[Fatigue Resistance (F) has been acquired. Your vitality has slightly enhanced.]


‘As expected, my guess was right. When the average stats are low, you have to keep pushing your body to its limits for resistances to form.’

What Ihyeon realized most desperately while suffering from the curse was the importance of resistance.

What if he had possessed S-class Cold Resistance at that time?

He could have raided the Snow Queen much more easily.

No, even if he had only possessed A-class Curse Resistance—

He wouldn’t have suffered that much.

But Ihyeon, like most people, had overlooked the importance of such resistances.

‘Because the process of gaining resistance is too painful.’

They knew it was necessary, but believed they could make up for it with items.

Then, only later do they realize it.

That there are things that can never be filled by items.

‘If you don’t have enough physical ability to support it, once you hit a wall, it becomes harder to climb higher. It means your capacity is lacking that much.’

Even if you try to gain resistance later, by then it’s already impossible.

Once your stats increase significantly, pushing beyond your limits becomes much harder.

But—

‘I’m different. While suffering from the curse, most of my stats were reset to a beginner’s level.’

That made his body ideal for acquiring resistances.

‘Just looking at my Cold Resistance and Curse Resistance, which are already S-class, the difference is quite clear.’


[The Yellow Wolf pack attempts to use ‘Curse: Fear Infection’!]

[Negated by Curse Resistance (S).]

[The Yellow Wolf pack attempts to use ‘Curse: Toxic Infiltration’!]

[Negated by Curse Resistance (S).]


Just seeing most of the Yellow Wolves’ skills bounce off showed how powerful resistance truly was.

In a way, this entire situation had become a blessing in disguise for Ihyeon.

The reason he wasn’t wearing any protective gear at all was exactly because of that.


[Strength has increased by 1.]

[Stamina has increased by 2.]


Bang!!

Ihyeon crushed the last remaining wolf’s head in an instant.

A blow far greater than when he first clashed with the wolf pack.

Crackle!

His lightning flared brilliantly.

‘Maybe it’s because my resistances have solidified, but my bodily senses have become much sharper too.’

It was the result of continuously observing Sun Wukong through the Fiery Golden Eyes.

However, when he tried applying what he had only understood in theory to actual combat, there were still quite a few things that needed correction.

‘If I only limit myself to imitating Sun Wukong, I won’t be able to grow stronger any faster than this.’

Ihyeon’s Fiery Golden Eyes burned with intensity.

‘But in the end, that would be like wearing clothes that don’t fit. I need to tear it all down and rebuild it in my own way.’

Ihyeon sought a middle ground between his own fighting style and Sun Wukong’s.

Revision.

Or reinterpretation.

Either way, that would be an accurate description.


[You have entered a state of ecstasy due to heightened madness. Combat Instinct (F) has advanced to grade E.]

[Your mana is surging. The mana circuit is overheating.]

[Stamina insufficient. Rest is recommended.]


“Haah! Haah!”

Sweetness lingered in his mouth.

But Ihyeon moved like a beast hunting its next prey.

He had to keep this feeling alive.

Until the quest ended.

He had deliberately spared one of them to let it escape.

Most likely, the place it ran off to was the den of the Yellow Wolves.

By raiding the den, he could not only train himself but also find clues to complete the quest.

‘Chase it.’

Fwoosh!

Ihyeon, his Fiery Golden Eyes blazing, began to follow the blood trail left by the wolf.
