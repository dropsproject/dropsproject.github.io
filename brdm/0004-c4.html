Chapter 4: Great Sage, Heaven’s Equal, Sun Wukong (1)

[You have successfully solved all the riddles!]

[The ritual has been completed.]


The moment Ihyeon stepped onto the altar and offered the ritual cup, a message appeared.

And then—


[The master of the royal tomb prepares for resurrection.]

[The hidden form of the royal tomb will be revealed!]


[Time remaining until the royal tomb is revealed.]

[00:15:00]

[00:14:59]

……


“Done!”

“Wow!”

The hunters’ faces brightened.

Because at last, it seemed the dangerous situation was finally over.

They were deeply grateful to Ihyeon, who had saved them from danger.

‘We used to be called top-class wherever we went…’

‘We really tried to show off in front of a swan.’

‘How could someone like that have never been known until now?’

Perhaps because relief was beginning to set in, they were finally realizing just how incredible Ihyeon’s performance had been.

His sharp sense of judgment in crisis—

His charisma that commanded the entire raid with just a few words—

His intellect that solved riddles in an instant, and even the sheer strength that overpowered monsters despite his weakened body.

The phrase “master of both pen and sword” came to mind.

‘How can we persuade him to join our guild?’

While their eyes sparkled as they looked at Ihyeon, just like Im Wonyoung’s had—

Meanwhile, Im Wonyoung herself could not bring herself to smile.

‘Is this really the end…?’

For some reason, she had the feeling it wouldn’t end here.

The difference from before was too great.

And sure enough.

Right at that moment.

Rumble!

The ground beneath their feet began to split apart with a violent quake.

“W-what is this!”

“The ground is splitting!”

“Be careful! The pit’s too deep!”

The forest collapsed, and stone monuments toppled like dominos. Hills rose and sank across the land.

The dungeon terrain was... changing.

At that instant—

Crack!

The altar where Ihyeon stood rose high into the air.

Chhhk.

『This is my final command.』

“……!?”

“Command…?”

Suddenly, Ihyeon’s voice came through the hunters’ brooches.

『The dungeon is about to collapse. Everyone must escape immediately. Head for the exit, in the opposite direction of where the zombie monkeys are running…』

『What are you saying all of a sudden, Hunter Jang! You’re telling us to escape now!?』

Im Wonyoung grabbed a nearby hunter’s brooch and shouted urgently.

She wanted to run to where Ihyeon was, but the tremors were too intense to move easily.

Still, Ihyeon remained calm.

『It seems this dungeon is designed to collapse automatically once the ritual is complete. Think about it. If the master of the tomb saw his people injured and used as sacrifices, what kind of reaction would he have?』

The hunters’ faces stiffened. Indeed, dungeons that self-destructed weren’t unheard of.

『Then what about you, Hunter Jang…!』

『A priest must prove their faith. If all the priests run away, the master’s wrath will only grow.』

『……!』

『My role is to appease the master of the tomb as much as possible. To buy time so the team can escape safely.』

Im Wonyoung was speechless.

She wanted to ask why he had to be the one to sacrifice himself, but—

『Didn’t we make a promise? That I’d clear the dungeon and ensure as many people as possible could make it out alive.』

『……!』

『Hurry. If we delay any longer, it’ll be too late.』

Just as Ihyeon said, the quakes grew even worse.

The ground of the boss room had completely sunk, and the hunters had already retreated to the outskirts.

“Team leader, we have to go now.”

“We can’t let Hunter Jang’s sacrifice be in vain. Hurry!”

The hunters rushed to her side, grabbing Im Wonyoung by the shoulders.

『Don’t worry. I’ll find a way to escape from here somehow. I’m a pathfinder, after all.』

Im Wonyoung’s lips trembled for a moment before her expression hardened. Suddenly, she threw something toward Ihyeon.

He caught it instinctively and froze.

It was a red jade ring pulsing with powerful magic.

‘A sacred relic…!’

『Please, make it out alive. I’ll bring a rescue team back as soon as I get out.』

『……Understood.』

Ihyeon nodded heavily.

Sacred relics were not something even family members shared lightly.

For her to entrust it to him like this could only mean one thing to Ihyeon, deep respect.

Ihyeon was truly grateful for Im Wonyoung’s kindness.

She gave him a faint smile and turned away. In that moment, her expression changed.

Her face grew stern.


[00:11:47]


There wasn’t much time left.

“Everyone, run to the exit. Move in full assault formation.”

“Roger that.”

“Roger that.”

The hunters began to run in the direction Ihyeon had shown them.

The injured were carried on the backs of the Jangsanbeoms that Im Wonyoung had summoned.

Im Wonyoung planned to return immediately with the rescue team once they made it out. After all, she was the one responsible for this raid.

For now, she had no choice but to leave the clearing to Ihyeon, since ensuring her team’s survival came first.

But she didn’t want to make the mistake of leaving every burden from start to finish to an outsider like him.

“……”

Ihyeon kept his eyes on them until they completely disappeared. The jade ring was still on his left index finger.

“……Are they all gone now.”

From his high vantage point, he could see the dungeon’s terrain shifting without pause.

Only now could Ihyeon finally relax a little and focus on watching the dungeon’s end.

Because this was a moment he didn’t want to share with anyone else.

It was an opportunity given by his brother.

‘It’s also true that I don’t know what other dangers might still be here.’

In truth, Ihyeon still felt a bit sorry toward White Burn.

After all, he was practically taking all the rewards from <Monkey Den>, which originally belonged to them.

But without the Fiery Golden Eyes, the key to the boss room, Monkey Den was a dungeon that would have failed to be cleared anyway.

So he intended to repay them later with results that would far exceed what he gained here.

After all, he didn’t want to repay the goodwill they, and Im Wonyoung, had shown him with deception.


[00:00:02]

[00:00:01]

[00:00:00]


‘It’s coming.’


[Countdown complete.]

[The royal tomb is revealed!]


Beneath the completely collapsed ground of the boss room, there was another hidden area.

A massive glass tube of astonishing width.

Inside it slept a gigantic golden monkey, seemingly dozens of meters tall.

Gooooo!

“……!”

Just looking at it was enough to make one’s heart drop from sheer magnitude and presence.

It felt like one should bow their head.

Instinct whispered the need to kneel.

A being of that scale could never be ordinary.

Only those who had reached the heavens could exude such overwhelming power.

People have a word for them.

They call them god!

“Sun… Wukong.”

The moment Ihyeon murmured the name of the tomb’s master—

Flash!

The golden monkey sleeping inside the glass tube opened its eyes.

It felt as if a pillar of light shot up toward the sky.

And then—

Psssshh…!

The golden monkey’s body began to scatter into tiny particles, swirling together as they gathered toward the altar where Ihyeon stood.

As the particles condensed, they formed a long staff.

A staff that shone with a radiant golden light, as if carved from the sun itself.


—the divine staff that suppresses all under heaven.

―Ruyi Jingu Bang, the Compliant Golden-Hooped Staff.


Ihyeon read the old Chinese characters engraved at the end of the staff and clenched his fist.

He had been chasing Sun Wukong for so long that there was no way he wouldn’t recognize it.

The symbol of Sun Wukong.

The Compliant Golden-Hooped Staff.


[Compliant Staff]

A sacred relic representing ■■■■ ■■■.

Originally crafted by Taishang Laojun for Yu, who earned great merit by taming the floods of the Yellow River. Later, it was taken by Sun Wukong from the Dragon King of the Eastern Sea and used as his weapon. Some claim it was once wielded by Pangu, the primordial giant.

—Type: Sacred Relic.

—Origin: Myth ‘Journey to the West’

*It possesses numerous abilities that reflect the many legends surrounding it, so many that even Sun Wukong himself could not unlock them all.

**Most of its functions now seem to be sealed for reasons unknown.


Ihyeon swallowed hard.

At last, the results of the past four years had borne fruit.

At the same time, a question he had set aside earlier resurfaced.

This place was clearly a tomb.

A burial ground honoring a dead king.

Then why was Sun Wukong buried in a place like this?

No, more than that, why were the words “Great Sage, Heaven's Equal, Sun Wukong” redacted from the Compliant Staff’s description?

‘Could there be more to the story of Journey to the West than I know?’

The moment that question crossed Ihyeon’s mind—

Flash!

A small cluster of light burst above the Compliant Staff, and a tiny baby monkey appeared.

It was adorable, covered from head to toe in pure white fur like an albino.

Yet for some reason, Ihyeon felt afraid of it.

「It has been such a long wait. I was wondering when someone worthy would finally appear… Hm?」

The baby monkey looked at Ihyeon and smiled brightly, but then tilted its head curiously.

After a moment, it narrowed its eyes.

「You… who are you?」


***


Goooooo!

Despite its cute appearance, its overwhelming pressure crushed the surroundings.

It was the same level of pressure he had felt when he saw the giant golden monkey.

No, maybe the level was the same, but the ferocity and savagery were far worse.

It felt as if it might devour Ihyeon right then and there.

「How could someone without ‘qualification’ have my ‘eyes’ and come all the way here?」

The baby monkey, Sun Wukong, growled.

The fangs glinting between its small lips shone with a cruel light.

Did it think Ihyeon had harmed the one who held the proper ‘qualification’ and stolen the Fiery Golden Eyes?

Before the pressure grew any stronger, Ihyeon lowered his head.

“I greet the Great Sage, Heaven's Equal, Sun Wukong. My name is Jang Ihyeon.”

「I’m not particularly asking who you are…!」

“I came in the stead of your original successor, following his final will.”

「Final will?」

A small crease formed between Sun Wukong’s cute brows.

The aura pressing down on Ihyeon subsided.

Ihyeon let out a silent sigh and calmly explained everything that had happened to him up to this point.

How much effort he had spent over the past four years searching for Sun Wukong’s traces.

The crease on Sun Wukong’s brow deepened.

「…So what you’re saying is, the one who was originally qualified to receive my ‘eyes’ sacrificed himself for you, and you received my ‘eyes’, then came here in his place. Is that it?」

“That’s correct.”

「And the reason you sought me out was to lift your curse?」

“Yes.”

Sun Wukong said nothing for a while.

That silence alone showed how deeply he was shaken by the fact that the one he had been waiting for was dead, and someone else had come instead.

「So it’s true. Ha, haha… So he really died like that? Just like that…」

Sun Wukong’s own Fiery Golden Eyes flared, then dimmed. He had confirmed whether Ihyeon was lying.

「You, do you even know what kind of duty comes with holding those ‘eyes’?」

“I don’t. I only know that the sage monkeys who granted the Fiery Golden Eyes had some kind of purpose.”

Sun Wukong let out a hollow laugh.

「We’re doomed.」

“……”

「This wasn’t supposed to happen at all. I can’t believe something like that happened there… damn it…」

Then he irritably scratched the back of his head.

For a moment, Ihyeon’s eyebrow twitched. It sounded to him as though Sun Wukong was belittling Mun Suchan’s death.

「What’s that look for? Ah, you think I was insulting your brother? Pah! So you’ve got some loyalty, is that it?」

A laugh that carried both mockery and admiration.

「That’s not it, so don’t worry. I’m just pissed because the situation right now is so fucked up. The one who really wants to cry right now is me.」

At that moment, Sun Wukong’s golden eyes filled with emptiness.

「My resurrection plan is already ruined before it even began. Ha ha…」

Resurrection?

In that instant, the pieces of Ihyeon’s long-held doubts clicked together as one.

“I apologize if this is rude to ask so suddenly—”

For some reason, Ihyeon felt that Sun Wukong’s situation might not be so different from his own.

“Were you by any chance murdered by someone?”
