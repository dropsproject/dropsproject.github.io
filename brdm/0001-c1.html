Chapter 1: Pathfinder Jang Ihyeon (1)

‘It’s still chilly.’

Although it had already been a week since March began, the cold wind that had blown all winter still showed no sign of fading.

White Burn.

Even though it was said to be the best guild in South Korea, they weren’t much different.

After all, no one wears items outside the dungeon.

‘If you ever did, you’d just be treated like an idiot.’

While Assistant Manager Min was idly wondering about that, and why equipment always looked like it came out of a cosplay—

A voice beside him startled him.

“I’m freezing to death out here, why is he taking so long?”

A tall woman who looked to be over 170 centimeters.

She was smoking a cigarette, her sharp eyes visible beneath her loosely tied hair.

Smoking in the park was, of course, illegal.

But among the thirty guild members present, no one pointed it out.

Rather, they all simply averted their eyes and pretended not to notice.

That was because she was not only the leader of this raid team but also the granddaughter of the guild master.

Im Wonyoung.

A talented ranker who had achieved that title at the young age of thirty-one.

Was this some kind of message hinting that she might be the next in line to lead?

‘Even though a hunter’s life is one where you never know when you’ll die, this feels a bit too much.’

Still, as typical company workers worn down by Korean corporate life, none of them had the courage to question her.

They just chose to pretend not to see.

‘That’s probably why, even though more than ten minutes remain before the actual meeting time, no one is saying anything. Or rather, they can’t?’

It was then that Assistant Manager Min glanced at his wristwatch, having that kind of common thought.

‘Hm?’

He noticed someone approaching the park and looked slightly surprised.

Judging by the large backpack, it seemed to be the man they were scheduled to meet today, but his face looked oddly familiar.

“What, I thought it was just a coincidence that the name was the same! But it really is you!?”

“Min Gyeong-su?”

That man, Ihyeon, was also surprised.

“Yeah, my man! Wow, after I heard you suddenly got discharged four years ago, I always wondered what you were up to…”

Assistant Manager Min trailed off, smiling awkwardly as he glanced down at Ihyeon.

Unlike them, neatly dressed in proper attire, Ihyeon looked shabby, as if he had pulled his clothes out of a donation bin.

“Well, what matters is that you’re doing fine. Anyway, it’s really good to see you.”

“Assistant Manager Min, is that pathfinder someone you know?”

At that moment, Im Wonyoung approached from behind, her heels clicking against the ground. The cigarette in her mouth had burned down quite a bit.

“Nice to meet you. I’m Jang Ihyeon.”

“I’m Im Wonyoung. We met briefly during the pre-meeting, right?”

Ihyeon shook her hand and nodded curtly.

“Yes. I remember you were with Team Leader Cha.”

“That’s right. Team Leader Cha gave a strong recommendation for you back then, so expectations are high even within the company.”

“I’ll do my best.”

“We’re not looking for effort. We’re looking for results.”

“Yes. I’ll make sure to deliver.”

Even under her constant pressure, Ihyeon didn’t flinch.

Im Wonyoung raised an eyebrow, her expression saying, “Well, look at that.” She looked amused.

“So, you and Assistant Manager Min are friends? Classmates? Elementary school? Middle school?”

“We were classmates at the Military Academy.”

“The Academy?”

Im Wonyoung’s eyes widened slightly.

The other hunters listening were also surprised. They couldn’t hide their looks that seemed to say, “him?”

And it made sense. The “Military Academy” Assistant Manager Min mentioned referred to the National Awakener Training Academy, an institution that specialized in training awakened individuals under government supervision.

Commonly called the Awakeners’ Academy, or simply the Academy.

It was known as a place reserved only for elites among aspiring hunters.

Even if graduates didn’t become commissioned officers, most were scouted by famous guilds or sent overseas.

But the man standing before them was just a "pathfinder."

Someone who mainly navigated dungeons, acted as a porter for the raid team during combat, or, in the worst cases, served as a decoy.

That meant he held the lowest rank within the raid team.

A graduate of the Academy doing that kind of work?

“This guy was one of the prides of our 17th class.”

When Assistant Manager Min spoke up, the suspicious gazes subsided somewhat.

But the hunters didn’t miss that he had used the past tense.

They could even sense a faint hint of mockery.

‘A loser, huh.’

‘Did he suffer mental contamination? Or maybe just PTSD?’

‘Whatever it is, I just hope he doesn’t cause us trouble inside.’

The dungeon they were assigned to this time was <Monkey Den>.

Although it was only a 2-star dungeon, it had remained unconquered for over a year since it was practically a labyrinth.

Since the losses could be considerable, they only hoped Ihyeon wouldn’t become a burden.

But unlike those uneasy thoughts around her, Im Wonyoung just smiled brightly.

“I’m an Academy graduate too. Class of the 15th. Didn’t expect to meet a fellow alum here.”

“Nice to meet you, senior.”

“Senior? We’re out in society now, so no need for that. Still, school ties aren’t a bad thing. Let’s work well together.”

Im Wonyoung’s impression of Ihyeon was favorable.

Although she had been grumbling about the cold, he had arrived exactly on time and didn’t seem intimidated by the intense atmosphere of the raid team.

“Alright then, let’s get going. We can talk more along the way.”

Following Im Wonyoung’s lead, Ihyeon and White Burn’s raiding party, “Great Tiger," stepped through the gate.

[Dungeon ‘Monkey Den’]

[You have entered a folktale filled with various kinds of monkeys.]

[Monkeys are clever and cunning. Overcome all their interference and clear the dungeon.]

[Upon success: Obtain ‘■■■■’ of ‘■■’.]

[Upon failure: Degenerate into a ‘Zombie Monkey’.]


Dungeons are usually based on the <stories> known to the public.

So the more stories you know, the more helpful it is in clearing a dungeon—

‘But when you don’t know what story it’s based on, it can really feel hopelessly frustrating.’

This was exactly why White Burn had hired Ihyeon separately instead of using the company’s internal exploration team.

‘At first, I wondered why we had to spend so much money on a pathfinder… Turns out there was a reason after all.’

Im Wonyoung glanced curiously at Ihyeon, who was clearing the knee-high grass with a jungle knife while scouting the area.

As expected, Ihyeon’s skills were clearly on a different level from the clumsy guides they had worked with before.

He was the kind of “real deal” that even Im Wonyoung, who was eager to fill her guild with capable people, found tempting.

“This way.”

Even when they reached a confusing fork, he made his choice without hesitation—

“Stop.”

And the moment he gave that signal, a trap was triggered as if on cue, keeping everyone safe by a hair’s breadth.

Even the assault of the monkey horde that had caused heavy casualties during a previous attempt was handled with ease this time.

“Wow, we’re not even wandering in circles. He’s spot on every time! Did he perhaps scout the dungeon beforehand?”

“Does that even make any sense? If he had, we would’ve cleared it already.”

“I’m just saying. Still, the more I watch him, the stranger it gets. Could his trait be prophecy or something?”

“If he were a prophet, do you think he’d be working as a pathfinder? He probably learned something useful at the academy.”

Even the team members who had doubted Ihyeon’s abilities at first were now watching him in awe.

A forest path winding like a maze.

Traps that triggered at every turn.

The sweet, honey-like scent of forest fruit that caused hallucinations.

And on top of that, zombie monkeys constantly trying to kidnap hunters.

Zombie monkeys had the habit of turning those they captured into zombies, making them their allies.

It was chilling to see zombies with the faces of former comrades suddenly appear from the trees.

Because of this, there had even been talk among the higher-ups about raising Monkey Den’s rating to 3-stars—

But for reasons unknown, that plan was always shelved, and new strike teams were sent in instead.

However—

‘This time, we’ve reached the mid-stage with zero casualties. It’s all thanks to Hunter Jang.’

And it hadn’t even been that difficult.

‘On the outside, he looks like a limping man with weak stamina.’

Im Wonyoung found it fascinating how Ihyeon avoided traps so effortlessly despite that limp.

‘And also, it was strange how a golden light sometimes flickers around the corners of his eyes. Some sort of skill? But I don’t sense any mana from it... Then it must be a divine authority, but I doubt a ranker would take on this kind of job.’

Im Wonyoung tilted her head, cutting her analysis short.

‘But Jang Ihyeon… Jang Ihyeon… Where have I heard that name before?’

“Assistant Manager Min.”

“Yes, Team Leader.”

Assistant Manager Min, who served as the team’s tank, came running.

‘Wow, she really is beautiful.’

Assistant Manager Min flinched slightly when Im Wonyoung suddenly leaned in close.

It was said that she had even received several offers from the entertainment industry because of her striking looks.

“By any chance, what was your relationship with Hunter Jang back in the academy?”

Suppressing a sudden twinge of jealousy, Assistant Manager Min forced a calm reply.

“Are you interested in him?”

“Talent is always welcome. You said he was the pride of your Class 17, wasn’t he?”

Assistant Manager Min scratched his cheek awkwardly.

“Yes. If you ask any of the 17th batch, there’s probably no one who doesn’t know him.”

“Don’t hold back. Tell me in detail.”

“Ah, this is classified information though…”

Classified?

Im Wonyoung’s eyes sparkled.

“Don’t tell me he was with Blue Thunder?”

The Blue Thunder Unit was a division only top graduates from the Awakeners' Military Academy could apply to.

“Ah, seriously, this mustn’t leave this spot. You have to keep it secret, Team Leader.”

Leaning in close, Assistant Manager Min whispered into Im Wonyoung’s ear.

“Do you remember the Gangneung Incident four years ago?”

“He was part of that team?”

“Not just part of it. He was the one in charge.”

“……!?”

Im Wonyoung’s eyes widened.

Now she remembered why the name had sounded so familiar.

“Then Jeteu (Jet)…?”

“Yes. That’s him.”

In that instant, Im Wonyoung’s eyes gleamed like someone who had just discovered a hidden treasure.


***


‘Jet. It’s been a while since I last heard that code name.’

Ihyeon smiled bitterly as he cleared the path ahead.

Although Im Wonyoung and Assistant Manager Min probably thought they were whispering quietly, sound carried too well around him at times like this, perhaps because of old habits.

In truth, Jet was a name given to him by Mun Suchan.

It meant to become a figure representing Blue Thunder, the Beullu Jeteu (Blue Jet).

It seemed the shadow of Mun Suchan had always lingered throughout his life.

‘Hyung.’

Ihyeon gently touched his own eyes with his left hand for a moment.

Eyes that gleamed with gold.

Fiery Golden Eyes.

Even after all these years, his brother’s warmth still protected him.

With that warmth as his foundation, Ihyeon had pursued the traitors who killed his brother.

For four long years, without missing a single day.

That was also the reason he turned down his mentor’s offer to become an instructor or professor at the Awakeners’ Military Academy.

At the same time—

He never stopped searching for a way to regain his strength so that he could personally deliver judgment when he finally met the traitors.

Of course, it wasn’t easy.

After all, with just modern medicine, the curse placed on him by the 4-star boss monster was almost impossible to cure.

However—

‘If a being of higher <Rank> could lift the curse, or replace it with another?’

Ihyeon believed that if it was the true holder of the Fiery Golden Eyes, Sun Wukong, then he could surely help him.

There were a few pieces of information he had heard from Mun Suchan.


—The master of the Fiery Golden Eyes is in a place connected to monkeys. Find him. With him by your side... you should be able to do anything.


So he put in a lot of effort to find Sun Wukong—

And finally—


[‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ is now active. The power’s effect is weak due to insufficient mana.]

[Fiery Golden Eyes detects a familiar presence in <Monkey Den>.]


It seemed he had found his trail right here.
