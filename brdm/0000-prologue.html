Prologue


Ihyeon thought that his life was like a pearl.

A pearl formed by filtering and refining mud.

Though he grew up as an orphan and endured every hardship imaginable, his life had always been one of unbroken glory.

Top of the 17th class of the Awakeners’ Military Academy upon admission, and an early graduate.

He rose to become the world’s youngest Ranker and even served as the leader of a special task force.

It was a life that could truly be said to shine as brightly as his dazzling career.

But……


[Has fallen into extreme fatigue.]

[Fatigue level: 91]

[Current status is ‘near death.’]


‘In this situation, would something like that even matter?’


[The curse of the <Snow Queen>, ‘Ring of Bitter Cold,’ forcibly shuts down the mana circuit.]

[The core has been damaged. Mana can no longer be controlled.]


A dungeon of absolute zero, where even breath froze in an instant.

The 4-star dungeon, <Ice Palace>, which appeared for the first time in twenty years, had descended into utter chaos after countless fierce battles.

‘Gyeong-ryeol.’

His friend, who was always busy grumbling, ended up with his heart pierced while protecting him.

‘Miryeong.’

The junior who said she respected him, though only two years younger, had the upper half of her body torn apart and froze to death.

They weren’t the only ones.

All around him, his squad members who once laughed and talked with him lay dead or gravely injured.

The 66th Special Operations Unit.

Known as the Awakened Unit, or Blue Thunder (Cheongnoe), the special force that once shone with glory had suffered a near-annihilating blow.


[Vision damage is too severe.]

[‘Curse of Bitter Cold’ has paralyzed the optic nerve. The state ‘Blindness (Maengmok)’ has been added.]


Ihyeon wanted to at least catch one last glimpse of his comrades, but his vision was being clouded by ice crystals, making it impossible to see his surroundings clearly.

The curse he had obtained after defeating the boss monster of the Ice Palace, the Snow Queen.

Even in death, she had not gone down easily.

“...Why did you do that?”

Ihyeon approached the person dearest to him.

Mun Suchan.

A fellow orphan like him, someone who had been with him for over twenty years, all the way from the orphanage to the Academy.

An older brother. No, more like a parent.

“Haa... Are you crying? Crying...? Damn… are you for real? A man... blubbering like that...”

But he had a dagger buried in his heart, coughing out his final breath.

The corners of his trembling lips were smiling.

He had always been someone who smiled a lot.

“Why did you do that!”

“Well... my stupid little brother... was about to die... so his hyung had to save him... if not me... then who else...?”

When Ihyeon had risked the destruction of his mana core to land the final blow on the Snow Queen—

He had never expected betrayal.

Four of his squadmates, tempted by the sacred relic dropped by the Snow Queen, had suddenly attacked him from behind.

It all happened so fast. He barely managed to take down two of them, but couldn’t stop the remaining two.

In the end, the relic was stolen, and he was left on the verge of death.

It was then that Mun Suchan stepped in.

He had taken the blade in Ihyeon’s place.

Before the surviving teammates could restrain the traitors, they fled.

The shock Ihyeon felt was immense, because one of the betrayers was his own lover.

“Damn it! You think I’d be grateful for something like this, huh?”

“Of course not... you ungrateful brat...”

“Just stay here for a bit. There should be healers outside the dungeon, I’ll bring them—!”

Mun Suchan grabbed Ihyeon’s wrist as he tried to leave.

“Don’t... go. It’s... too late for me anyway...”

“That might be so, but it’s not! I’ll get you out of here somehow and...!”

“You... can’t see, can you?”

For a moment, Ihyeon froze.

Mun Suchan’s smile deepened.

Golden eyes.

Golden Eye.

His eyes, which also served as his codename, curved into arcs.

“Anyway... you really can’t lie... That’s why you keep getting... taken advantage of so easily. I told you, didn’t I...? Not to trust people... too easily... I said that she wasn’t...”

“Fine, fine. I get it, so stop talking! I’m getting the healer!”

“Take my eyes...”

Mun Suchan weakly lifted his head.

In the mirror-like gold of his pupils, Ihyeon’s tear-streaked face was reflected.

“What are you even talking about—”

“You know what my eyes are, right...? It’s the end for me anyway... but not for you... not yet.”

Though it was not publicly known, Mun Suchan’s eyes were a sacred relic.

The Fiery Golden Eyes.

Eyes that reveal the truth, said to hold the saga of the Great Sage, Sun Wukong.

Mun Suchan had claimed he gained this power when he was just a child, while playing in a mountain cave.

He said he received it as a gift after playing with sage monkeys.

Ever since then, he had always been Ihyeon’s steadfast ally.

Always leading the way, scouting ahead, and checking for enemy ambushes.

It would be no exaggeration to say that the fate of the entire unit was in his hands.

Pathfinder.

The Guide.

Mun Suchan often said, like a habit, that he was proud his eyes could always help Ihyeon.

“Don’t say crap like that! I’m not taking them even if you gave them to me, so get up already!”

“Live for both of us. Live well.”

“Hyung!”

“Try to live more while saving a bit...”

Slowly, Mun Suchan’s eyes began to close.

“No! Open your eyes! Please!”

Ihyeon poured every potion he had into him and screamed at his remaining teammates to bring reinforcements already.

“But... little brother... I thought for sure you were handsome, but... up close, you’re kind of ugly...”

Thud.

Then Mun Suchan went limp, still wearing that faint smile.

Ihyeon’s cries filled the dungeon.

And then—

Four years passed.
