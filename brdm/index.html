Synopsis:

Top graduate of the military academy, the world’s youngest Ranker, and leader of the awakened unit, Blue Thunder—

Jang Ihyeon.

He lost both his sight and his powers in a 4-star dungeon that appeared for the first time in decades.

“Take my eyes… you know what they are, don’t you?”

“What are you talking about?!”

“Find ‘him’. If it’s that person, he’ll grant you great strength.”

With the Fiery Golden Eyes he inherited from his brother, Ihyeon spent four years traversing dungeons in search of ‘him’.

At last, he discovered the dungeon where he was sealed…

“Who are you? Why do you bear the power of my apostle?”

Will Ihyeon be acknowledged by the true owner of the eyes, The Great Sage, Heaven's Equal, Sun Wukong—

To regain his lost strength and reunite his scattered unit?

“I wish to become your successor, Great Sage. Please, grant me the honor.”
