Chapter 5: Great Sage, Heaven’s Equal, Sun Wukong (2)

From the very moment he realized that the owner of the royal tomb was Sun Wukong, Ihyeon couldn’t shake the feeling that something was off.

At the end of Journey to the West, Sun Wukong became a Buddha equal to the Jade Emperor and the Buddha himself.

A Buddha is also a god.

And gods do not die.

That is why they are called immortal beings (bulmyeolja).

However, the death of a god was not entirely impossible.

‘If a god were to suffer a crushing defeat in battle against another god, or if the myth sustaining their existence were to collapse… then, in theory, it would be possible.’

And Ihyeon had personally witnessed the death of a god.

‘The first 5-star dungeon incident.’

Six years ago, when Ihyeon had just joined the Blue Thunder Unit, a 5-star dungeon appeared in South Africa.

It marked the occurrence of a <Large-scale Myth>, something previously thought to exist only in theory.

‘At that time, Europe and the United States poured in their forces just to barely seal the gate.’

The dungeon suppression operation finally ended only after four of the world’s nine Top Rankers stepped forward.

Ihyeon had been there, dispatched as part of the peacekeeping force.

‘That was also the moment I decided to become a true ranker, to dedicate my life to being a hunter.’

A longing to stand on the same battlefield as humanity’s strongest.

And an ambition to one day surpass them, to challenge the divine itself.

Although he had set that dream aside for a time after meeting the Snow Queen—

The passion he once felt still lingered as an ember in his heart.

‘Come to think of it, one of the nine thousand who participated back then was Team Leader Im Wonyoung’s grandmother.’

At any rate, if Sun Wukong had died for a similar reason.

Then it made sense for him to be laid to rest in such a tomb.

In other words—

‘The Sun Wukong here must be the remnant of his fallen status...’

And when he thought about it that way, Sun Wukong did seem a little less overwhelming than his legendary reputation suggested.


[You have uncovered part of the secret that ■■■■ was hiding. The ability of ‘Sacred Relic: Fiery Golden Eyes’ has slightly increased.]


「Who got murdered?! If those bastards hadn’t suddenly backstabbed us...!」

Sun Wukong flared up but quickly realized his mistake and shut his mouth.

By then, Ihyeon was certain.

Someone had struck down Sun Wukong.

The Great Sage, Heaven’s Equal, Sun Wukong himself.

If that was the case—

"This tomb and the arrangements surrounding it must have been created by the monkeys of Mount Huaguo to resurrect the Great Sage as a god. The Fiery Golden Eyes were a tool meant to be given to the apostle who would aid in that resurrection."

「…….」

When the myths that make up a god scatter, their rank naturally falls, even reducing them to mere mortals.

Perhaps Apostle Mun Suchan’s duty had been “the resurrection of Sun Wukong,” or “his return as a god.”

As Mun Suchan carried out his destined task, the scattered myths would have gradually gathered once more.

In other words, completing the arrangements meant completing Sun Wukong’s resurrection.

‘In that sense, the Fiery Golden Eyes are the perfect sacred relic for it. You’d need sharp eyes to find the scattered myths.’

It was clear how much thought the monkeys of Mount Huaguo had put into it.

‘But that apostle has disappeared.’

It was only natural that Sun Wukong would react so anxiously.

‘Then who in the world managed to kill Sun Wukong?’

Even before becoming a Buddha, Sun Wukong had fought the Jade Emperor’s heavenly army and survived unscathed.

Someone like that couldn’t have been brought to this state by just anyone.

At that moment, Ihyeon saw an opening.

"May I ask who the enemies were that harmed the Great Sage?"

Sun Wukong remained silent for a moment.

His displeasure was clear.

「Why? What will you do if you find out? Will you laugh at how low I’ve fallen?」

"Of course not. Maybe I might even be able to help?"

「You? And how would you possibly do that?」

His tone dripped with sarcasm, but Ihyeon answered calmly.

"I may not be someone chosen by your subjects, Great Sage. But I’ve made it here entirely through my own strength. I believe that’s proof enough of my ability."

「…….」

"So, is there any way I could possibly help?"

Sun Wukong frowned but couldn’t refute his words.

After all, they were true.

Even more so because Ihyeon wasn’t the original chosen bearer of the Fiery Golden Eyes.

Finally, after a long silence—

Sun Wukong’s voice sank low.

「…The main gods (jusin). The Jade Emperor, Zeus, Odin, and those kinds of beings are the ones who did this to me.」

"……!"

The weight behind his words was by no means light.

「If they hadn’t disguised their malice as kindness and set their trap, if only I had seen through it and prepared properly, I would never have suffered such humiliation.」

"……."

「They crushed everything. My existence, my home, my kingdom, my people, my friends, my brothers, everything.」

Ihyeon pressed his lips tightly together.

The resentment seeping from Sun Wukong’s twisted lips was so deeply buried that it made one’s chest tighten in pain.

「Tripitaka always said it. That all things are emptiness (gong), and thus meaningless. But to me, they were not. They were everything that filled the void within my heart. I can never, ever forgive them.」

"……."

「So tell me, boy. After all that, what help can you possibly offer me? If you’re just asking out of idle curiosity, then I’ll—」

"Perhaps I can help, at least a little."

「What?」

At that moment, Sun Wukong saw it.

Ihyeon, who had been listening intently all along, now had a twisted smile at the corner of his lips.

He was enjoying this.

"One of the traitors who tried to kill me and your apostle was the Jade Emperor’s apostle."

「……!」

In Sun Wukong’s widening golden eyes, Ihyeon burned brightly like a raging flame.

"And I know where they are and what they’ve done. If you use me, Great Sage, it could benefit you as well."

For a brief instant, Tripitaka’s words echoed through Sun Wukong’s mind.

Karma is like the net of heaven, woven so tightly that everything is bound to connect in the end…

Could it really be coincidence that someone tied to his mortal enemy, the Jade Emperor, also shared a grudge with the boy before him?

Destiny.

That single word sank deeply into Sun Wukong’s heart.

Without realizing it, he let out a laugh.

「So you’re saying you’re offering yourself to be used?」

"Yes. In return, please heal my body."

「A trade, then?」

"That’s right."

「You’ve got quite the confidence. Very well, let’s see if you deserve to speak that way.」

Sun Wukong leapt down from the Compliant Staff.

「Receive its recognition. Do that, and I’ll acknowledge you as my new apostle.」

Ihyeon nodded and slowly approached the staff.

「For reference, this one’s temperament is rather nasty. It might be nothing like the gentle Fiery Golden Eyes you know of.」


[‘Event: Acknowledgement of the Compliant Staff’ has begun!]

[The spirit of ■■■■ wishes to grant you a temporary quest to become his apostle.]

[From this moment, undergo the trial of the Compliant Staff and earn acknowledgment.]

[Upon success: Apostleship of ■■■■.]

[Upon failure: Injury.]


Ihyeon knew full well that during the process of being chosen by a sacred relic, fatal accidents sometimes occurred under alarming circumstances.

All the more so for a relic with a great legacy like the Compliant Staff.

But if he had not been prepared for that, he would not have come this far.

The moment Ihyeon took a deep breath and reached out his hand—

Clink!

The Compliant Staff suddenly began to dissolve, its form collapsing as it slithered up Ihyeon’s arm like a snake.

Clack!

Before he realized it, a golden bracelet had formed around his wrist.

And then—

“...Hup!”

An overwhelming surge of divine power poured into his body.

It felt similar to when he first received the Fiery Golden Eyes transplant, but this energy was far rougher, almost violently so.

It felt as though his body might break apart at any moment.

Ihyeon realized that this was already the Compliant Staff’s “trial.”

Tap, tap, tututut!

In an instant, both of his eyes turned bloodshot.

‘Like I’d lose!’

Ihyeon clenched his teeth and tried to guide the divine power of the Compliant Staff in the direction he desired.

After all, he had once reached the level of a ranker, and when it came to controlling mana, he was confident.

‘But I can’t do it the way I used to. Divine power is a concept far beyond mana. In that case...!’

Recalling something, Ihyeon activated the Fiery Golden Eyes once again.

Fwoooosh!

At the same time, the flow of mana swirling around Sun Wukong became clearly visible to him.

‘Follow its owner’s flow!’


[Attempting to control divine power using the karma absorbed from ■■■■!]


「Oh, look at that? You’re trying to imitate me?」

While Sun Wukong was momentarily impressed—

Kwa-deudeudeuk!

Even the divine power flinched in surprise and reacted violently. The more it raged, the harder Ihyeon tried to assert control.

At that tense moment of standoff, when neither side yielded an inch—

Paaat!


[‘Sacred Relic: Amulet of Protection’ is activated!]


Suddenly, a red light streamed out from the jade ring Im Wonyoung had given him and seeped into his body.

‘The treasure Team Leader Im gave me…!’


[Amulet (Talisman) of Protection (Tiger Deity)]

Since ancient times, it has been said that those devoured by tigers became vengeful spirits, following the tiger even after death and serving to protect it.

Naturally, generals and great heroes who possessed exceptional martial prowess became even greater warriors, guarding the tiger.

—Type: Sacred Relic.

—Origin: Legend “Traditional Mountain Lord Folklore”


[The four Gulgaks and the one Iol dwelling within the relic wish to assist you in accordance with the tiger deity's command.]


The vengeful spirits embedded in the ring stepped forward to help Ihyeon.

Although they were of a lower rank compared to the divine power of the Compliant Staff, they were still sacred relics, and thus proved to be of great help to Ihyeon.

‘Get in!’

Control had completely shifted to Ihyeon.

Bang!

Paaaaah!

Divine power flowed smoothly through his wide mana circuit.

「I can’t fully acknowledge you yet, but I’ll keep watching the situation for now. Something like that, huh?」

Sun Wukong laughed cheerfully.

After all, Ihyeon had indeed proven himself, just as he said he would.


[‘Sacred Relic: Compliant Staff’ has read the karma of ■■■■ implanted within you and has acknowledged you as a ‘temporary candidate.’]

……

[Accumulate more of ■■■■’s karma and complete the event to be reborn as an apostle of ■■■■. The more of ■■■■’s karma you recover, the more hidden functions of the Compliant Staff you will be able to access.]

[Temporary apostle authority has been granted.]

[Physical restoration is commencing.]


‘No way?’

The moment Ihyeon’s eyes widened—


[The Compliant Staff channels its divine power to dispel the curse ‘Ring of Bitter Cold’ cast by the <Snow Queen>.]

[Treatment in progress: 1, 2, 3%……]


“……!!”

At the same time his fatigue faded, Ihyeon was astonished by the changes occurring within his mana circuits.

Until now, even when he had absorbed small traces of Sun Wukong’s karma, it had only untangled his twisted circuits. But now, their functions were actually being restored…!

He could even feel his damaged core being repaired.

Though it was only a faint amount, mana began to flow through the once-dried circuits like a small stream.

‘Ah.’

It was the mana he had regained after four years.
